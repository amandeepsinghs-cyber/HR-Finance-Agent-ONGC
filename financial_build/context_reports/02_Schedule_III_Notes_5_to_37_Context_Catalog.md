# ONGC Schedule III Notes to Accounts (Notes 5–37) Context Catalog (`FY17–FY26`)

This catalog documents the audited Schedule III Notes across the **Balance Sheet (Notes 5–23)**, **Statement of Profit & Loss (Notes 24–37)**, and **Cash Flow Statement (Ind-AS 7)**, linking exact numbers to the operational and financial reasons behind their movements.

---

## Part A: Balance Sheet Notes (Notes 5–23)

### Note 5: Oil & Gas Assets and Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE)
- **FY26 Balance:** `₹2,04,030 Cr` (`₹1,84,210 Cr` Oil & Gas Assets + `₹19,820 Cr` Other PPE) vs `₹1,93,970 Cr` in FY25 (`+5.2% YoY`).
- **10-Year Trajectory (`FY17–FY26`):** Expanded `+58.6%` from `₹1,28,672 Cr` in FY17 to `₹2,04,030 Cr` in FY26.
- **What Happened (Annual Report Context):** Capitalization of **KG-DWN-98/2 Cluster-2** deepwater subsea manifolds, FPSO risers, and **Mumbai High North/South Phase-IV** platforms added `₹34,240 Cr` of gross producing assets over FY25–FY26, offset by `₹24,180 Cr` annual unit-of-production depletion.

### Note 6: Capital Work-in-Progress (CWIP) & Intangible Assets Under Development (Exploratory Wells in Progress)
- **FY26 Balance:** `₹57,490 Cr` (`₹41,650 Cr` Development CWIP + `₹15,840 Cr` Exploratory Wells in Progress) vs `₹61,400 Cr` in FY25 (`-6.4% YoY`).
- **What Happened (Annual Report Context):** CWIP declined by `-₹3,910 Cr` in FY26 as Cluster-2 development wells in KG-DWN-98/2 achieved commercial first oil and transferred into Note 5 Producing Oil & Gas Assets, while Daman Upside Development (`₹7,800 Cr`) and Western Offshore pipeline replacement projects remained in active CWIP.

### Note 8: Non-Current Investments (Group Subsidiaries & JVs)
- **FY26 Balance:** `₹1,18,450 Cr` (`24.7%` of Total Assets) vs `₹1,14,920 Cr` in FY25 (`+3.1% YoY`) and `₹48,910 Cr` in FY17 (`+142.2%` over 10 years).
- **Two Structural Shifts in the Decade:**
  1. **FY18 (`+₹38,242 Cr`):** Acquisition of `51.11%` stake in **HPCL (`₹36,915 Cr`)**.
  2. **FY25 (`+₹15,075 Cr`) & FY26 (`+₹3,530 Cr`):** Conversion of OPaL Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) and fresh equity infusion totaling `₹18,365 Cr` (making **OPaL a `95.69%` subsidiary**), plus **`₹3,530 Cr` equity infusion into ONGC Green Limited (OGL)** for 2.1 GW renewable solar/wind acquisitions.

### Notes 10–13: Current Assets (Inventories, Trade Receivables, Cash & Bank, Other Current Assets)
- **FY26 Balance:** `₹62,740 Cr` (`₹9,840 Cr` Inventories, `₹11,920 Cr` Trade Receivables, `₹28,450 Cr` Cash & Bank Balances including OIDB Site Restoration Fund deposits, and `₹12,530 Cr` Other Current Assets).
- **What Happened (Annual Report Context):** Trade Receivable Days improved to **`33.1 days`** (`₹11,920 Cr`) on automated LC-backed collections from PSU refineries (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, MRPL) and GAIL. Cash & Bank expanded `+9.9%` to **`₹28,450 Cr`**, giving ONGC a **negative Net Debt (Net Cash) position of `-₹21,560 Cr`**.

### Notes 14–15: Equity Share Capital & Other Equity (Net Worth)
- **FY26 Balance:** **`₹3,34,190 Cr`** (`₹6,290 Cr` Equity Share Capital across `1,258.03 Cr` shares of `₹5` face value + `₹3,27,900 Cr` Other Equity / Retained Earnings) vs `₹3,16,802 Cr` in FY25 (`+5.5% YoY`) and `₹1,85,538 Cr` in FY17.
- **What Happened (Annual Report Context):** Retained earnings increased by `₹17,388 Cr` after distributing **`₹15,410 Cr`** in total dividends (`₹12.25/share`, representing a `46.8%` payout ratio).

### Notes 16, 17, 20: Borrowings & Ind-AS 116 Lease Liabilities
- **FY26 Balance:** Total Borrowings stood at **`₹6,890 Cr`** (`₹4,180 Cr` Non-Current Borrowings + `₹2,710 Cr` Short-Term Working Capital/Current Maturities) vs `₹7,412 Cr` in FY25 (`-7.0% YoY`) and peak standalone debt of `₹25,592 Cr` in FY18.
- **Lease Liabilities (Note 17 / Ind-AS 116):** Non-current and current lease liabilities of `₹12,950 Cr` represent multi-year charter-hire contracts for offshore drilling rigs, FPSOs, and offshore supply vessels (OSVs).

### Note 18: Non-Current Provisions (Decommissioning & Site Restoration Liability)
- **FY26 Balance:** `₹41,250 Cr` vs `₹38,940 Cr` in FY25 (`+5.9% YoY`).
- **What Happened (Annual Report Context):** Represents the present value of estimated future abandonment and site restoration costs for `580+` offshore platforms and `8,400+` sub-surface wells under Ind-AS 37, backed by dedicated **Site Restoration Fund (SRF)** deposits held with SBI under Section 33ABA of the Income Tax Act.

## Part B: Statement of Profit & Loss Notes (Notes 24–37)

### Note 24: Revenue from Operations (`₹1,31,492 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹1,37,846 Cr` in FY25)
- **Product Breakdown (FY26):**
  - **Crude Oil (`₹92,140 Cr`, `70.1%` share):** Nominated + JV crude sales of `19.45 MMT` at `$73.80/bbl` average realization (`-4.0% YoY` vs `$76.90/bbl` in FY25).
  - **Natural Gas (`₹27,480 Cr`, `20.9%` share):** Sales across APM gas (`$6.50/MMBTU` ceiling) and **New Well Gas (NWG, `24.1%` volume share at `$7.80/MMBTU`, a 20% premium over APM)**.
  - **Value-Added Products — VAP (`₹11,872 Cr`, `9.0%` share):** LPG, Naphtha, C2-C3 (Ethane-Propane for OPaL), and ATF/SKO from Uran and Hazira processing complexes.

### Note 25: Other Income (`₹10,450 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹9,982 Cr` in FY25)
- **Breakdown:** Dividend income of `₹5,620 Cr` from subsidiaries/JVs (HPCL, Petronet LNG, GAIL, OIL) + `₹4,110 Cr` interest income on `₹28,450 Cr` treasury/SRF deposits + `₹720 Cr` forex/miscellaneous gains.

### Note 26: Production, Transportation, Selling & Statutory Levies (`₹57,102 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹61,018 Cr` in FY25)
- **1. Statutory Levies (`₹31,842 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹36,448 Cr` in FY25, `-12.6% YoY`):**
  - **Royalty (`₹16,420 Cr`):** `20%` on onshore crude, `10%` on offshore crude, `10%` on natural gas (`-₹1,126 Cr YoY` due to softer Brent).
  - **OIDB Cess (`₹15,422 Cr`):** `20%` ad-valorem on nominated crude (`-₹240 Cr YoY`).
  - **SAED Windfall Tax (`₹0 Cr` vs `₹3,240 Cr` in FY25):** Completely eliminated following Govt notification on December 2, 2024.
- **2. Production, Transportation & Selling Expense (`₹25,260 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹24,570 Cr` in FY25, `+2.8% YoY`):**
  - Driven by chemical EOR polymer/alkali-surfactant injection in Mehsana (`₹1,840 Cr`), workover rig operations in Ankleshwar/Assam (`₹3,920 Cr`), and subsea pipeline integrity maintenance in Mumbai High (`₹4,680 Cr`).

### Note 28: Exploration Costs Written Off (`₹7,320 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹6,812 Cr` in FY25)
- **Breakdown:** Survey Costs (`₹1,840 Cr` 2D/3D seismic acquisition in Andaman, Mahanadi, and Ultra-Deepwater KG) + Exploratory Well Write-offs (`₹5,480 Cr` across `3` deepwater dry wells in KG Offshore, `2` wells in Kutch-Saurashtra, and `4` wells in Assam-Arakan under Successful Efforts Accounting).

### Note 30: Depreciation, Depletion, Amortization & Impairment (`₹24,180 Cr` in FY26 vs `₹22,745 Cr` in FY25)
- **Breakdown:** Depletion of producing Oil & Gas Assets (`₹19,240 Cr`, `+7.1% YoY` on full-year KG-98/2 Cluster-2 capitalization), Depreciation of processing plants/pipelines (`₹2,890 Cr`), Ind-AS 116 Right-of-Use (ROU) rig/vessel amortization (`₹1,730 Cr`), and minor asset impairment (`₹320 Cr`).
