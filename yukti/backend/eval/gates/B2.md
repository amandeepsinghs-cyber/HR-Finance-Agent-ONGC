## Known limitation: strict follow-ups 46.7 %
The follow-up answer key is dominated by multi-step analytical "why / so what" questions (e.g. *"why have transfer cycles left the North-East below strength?"* → net postings in vs out FY22–FY26; *"which fields improved EBITDA margin despite falling production?"*). The planner answers the measurable part correctly (filters, entity, period carried from the previous spec) but these need composite analyses (deltas between periods, ratios against a second population) that a single QuerySpec does not express, so the headline number is often missing. **Planned remedy:** B4 insight engine (delta vs last year, vs target/sanctioned, concentration, outliers, named exceptions) and the narrator limited to computed facts; region-vs-region comparison and executive-only post counts are tracked as follow-ups.

- **G4 round-1 fixes** made the planner fall back (rather than widen the question) when a filter cannot be expressed, which moved a few roots to the legacy path (root lenient 36 → 35) and lifted follow-ups (24 → 27).
