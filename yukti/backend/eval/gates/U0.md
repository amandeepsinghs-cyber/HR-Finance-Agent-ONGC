**Scorers.** *Lenient* = `run_eval.py` `overall_pass` (schema valid ∧ refusal correct ∧ right tool ∧ citation ∧ "rows or numbers returned"); it is comparable with the 2026-10-20 full sweep but cannot detect wrong numbers. *Strict* = lenient ∧ **anchor check**: at least one significant headline number from the frozen answer key (`data_bank/answer_key/INDEX.md`; years, FY labels, dates and integers < 10 excluded) must appear in the answer (spoken/display text, insights, KPIs, table or chart cells) within ±1.5 %. Refusals, policy-only and stress items have no anchor and count on the lenient criteria only. Latencies are inflated by concurrency 6 (single-turn p50 is ~5–6 s).

## Gate rule for later phases
A B-phase gate passes "no regression" when, on this same subset with cache OFF: strict pass (all) ≥ 52.5 %, refusal ok ≥ 114/120, schema ok ≥ 115/120, and no new exception class. B2's own targets (root ≥ 80 %, follow-ups ≥ 60 %) are measured on the **strict** scorer.
