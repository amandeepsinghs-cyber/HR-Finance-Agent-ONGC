## 4. Deviations / open points (for R-O review)

1. **Row-count targets (packet §7: P&L ≥ 30, BS ≥ 60):** ONGC prints 26–27 standalone P&L value lines and 43–48 standalone BS value lines (consolidated 43–46 / 52–63). Every printed line is captured, with 0 unparsed value lines. The thresholds are not achievable without inventing lines, so tests assert ≥ 26 / ≥ 43 / ≥ 40 (CF ≥ 40 is met: 54–71). **Needs O to confirm the lowered thresholds.**
2. **Cash tie method:** standalone FY24–FY26 tie directly. 9 combinations (all consolidated except FY17, plus standalone FY19 and FY21) tie only after the AR's printed "Less: Cash Credit/Bank OD" line in the C&CE components table: CF cash = BS C&CE − overdraft. `cash_tie_method` records `direct` or `net_of_overdraft (line N)`. Both numbers are printed; nothing is computed into the data.
3. **Unlabelled printed numbers kept, not dropped:** (a) CF detail|block-total sub-columns. The block total (e.g. 446,063.32 "Adjustments for") becomes its own row `[subtotal] <heading>` with `notes='unlabelled_block_subtotal'` (64 rows). (b) Unlabelled total lines (AR FY22 BS has no "Total non-current assets" label; consolidated attribution sums; CF check figure under closing cash) are kept as `[unlabelled line]` (113 rows), and each one is verified as a sum or as a repeat of an earlier total. (c) 4 rows that pdftotext split over two lines are merged (`split_row_merged:<line>`).
4. **Opening column `fiscal_year`:** the 1-Apr column gets `period_type='opening'` and the FY it opens (1-Apr-2022 → FY23), with `period_end` kept as printed. P1.6 can remap this if O prefers FY22 closing.
5. **Side-tab rule:** same as P1.2. Values are the trailing 2-dp tokens; known chapter-tab words right of column 100 are stripped, and no-value lines starting right of column 100 are skipped. There is no hard cut at column 165, because AR FY26 values run to column ~175.
6. **Note No. edge cases:** `48.4` and `58.10` (2-dp note numbers in the note column, AR FY17 P&L, AR FY25 consolidated BS) are recognised by position, not taken as values.
7. **Notes are not gated like statements.** 619/975 note subtotals are reproduced. The 356 unreproduced flags come from multi-group tables (provisions movement, lease movement, CSR break-ups), "Less:" blocks, and text tables. 63 lines (AR FY26 20, AR FY25 13, AR FY17 13, others ≤ 5) could not be split into columns. All are listed with page and line in `notes_parse_issues.csv` for O's Gemini fallback. The anchor values in §3 are unaffected.
8. **Not yet extracted:** price realisation (Directors' Report, `fact_price_realisation`) and the tax-reconciliation statutory rate as a dedicated row (the tax note's tables are parsed as `note_39_*` where a date header exists). Consolidated notes are out of scope. The production table exists only in AR FY26 in the ONGC|JV form.
9. **File count:** 5 code/config files + 8 one-page fixtures + this evidence file (packet max 6 counts the fixture directory as one entry). `dim_financial_line.csv` is not touched; mapping to `line_code` is O's job. Rows carry `section_path` and `line_seq` to support it.
10. **Labels:** `raw_label` excludes the roman prefix (in `roman_ref`) and the Note No. (in `note_ref`), and keeps formula text such as `(III-IV)`. The raw printed line is in `source_text`.
