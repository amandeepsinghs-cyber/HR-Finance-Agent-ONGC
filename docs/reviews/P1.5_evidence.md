## 6. Deviations and open items
1. **Scope beyond the packet (per D-45 and the parent brief):** the NSE provider and fetcher were added, even though packet §4 said not to build them. More than 5 files changed because of this.
2. **Beta still needs weekly prices.** The FY-end closes cover E (market cap) only. A 2-year weekly beta needs about 104 Fridays × 2 files per FY (about 1,200 NSE requests for FY15–FY26). `NseArchiveFetcher` can do it, one date at a time, but it was not run in order to keep volume low. Until Yahoo works or the sponsor approves a weekly NSE pull, SPEC-02 uses the industry-relevered beta fallback.
3. NSE closes are **unadjusted** (`adj_close`=None). This is correct for market cap. Weekly returns built from them would ignore dividends; the bonus issues are before FY17, so splits are not an issue.
4. The real Yahoo JSON structure is assumed to match the documented `timeseries.result[].meta.type` envelope; the parser also accepts the packet's flat form. Packet stop-and-ask trigger: compare when real files arrive.
