# P0.10 evidence — voice self-interruption hotfix (BUG-17, BUG-18)

**Step:** P0.10 (sponsor-requested hotfix, 23-Oct) · **Branch:** `v3/P0-stabilise` · **Commits:** `8a95da5` P0.10a stopgap + P0.10 stage 2 · **Status:** pre-G4 · **Spec:** SPEC-11 §6.3, BEH-07

## Result
**Yukti's own voice can no longer interrupt her.** Mic audio is half-duplex in two independent places, and interrupting is now deliberate:

| Layer | Change |
|---|---|
| **Browser gate** (`services/voiceGate.ts` `micGateOpen`, `page.tsx` mic callback) | No PCM is sent while Yukti audio is playing, nor for **300 ms** after playback ends (echo tail). A deliberate interrupt re-opens the gate immediately; it closes again when the next reply starts playing. |
| **Backend gate** (`live_session.py` `_mic_gate_open`) | Mic frames (binary and JSON paths) are dropped while Gemini is **generating**, during a **speak** turn, and until the **estimated end of browser playback** (sum of sent 24 kHz PCM durations) **+ 300 ms**. A browser `interrupt` sets `_barge_in_open`, which forwards the user's speech until the server `interrupted`/`turn_complete`. Dropped frames are counted (`_mic_frames_dropped`). |
| **Live VAD** (`_build_live_config`) | `realtime_input_config.automatic_activity_detection.start_of_speech_sensitivity = START_SENSITIVITY_LOW` (google-genai types present in the venv). |
| **Deliberate interrupt** | **Orb tap / mic button / Space / Esc** while Yukti speaks → `interruptNow()`: stop playback, send `interrupt`, open the gate, and keep (or start) listening, so a tap never just turns the mic off mid-answer. While speaking, the mic button becomes the **Stop** control (square icon, label "Stop Yukti speaking"). No new buttons were added. |
| **Voice barge-in** (`BargeInDetector`) | The old trigger (raw level > 0.5 for 200 ms, which fired on echo) is replaced. It now needs **~0.4 s cumulative** (leaky, survives syllable dips) of mic level above **max(0.6, learned echo floor + 0.25, 0.45 + 0.4 × playback level)**. The echo floor is an EMA of sub-threshold mic level during playback, so speech never inflates it. On trigger, the last ~0.6 s of gated frames are flushed after the `interrupt`, so Live hears the start of the user's sentence. |
| **speak_id** (BUG-17/18) | The UI sends `speak {text, speak_id}`. Backend: `interrupt` **no longer clears** `_is_speak_only`. Only that speak's own `turn_complete` (carrying `speak_id`) or a server `interrupted` (also carrying `speak_id`) ends it, and its transcripts are dropped until then. Frontend `SpeakTracker`: `agent_transcript` is ignored while a speak (REST answer, which already has its bubble) is active and after `turn_complete` until the next user turn, so there are no orphan/fragment bubbles and no contract + transcript concatenation. A stale completion of an older speak never ends a newer one. |

## Manual live test (sponsor, laptop speakers, ~10 min)
Hard-reload the UI (Ctrl+Shift+R) on `http://localhost:3300`.
1. **No self-interrupt (×5):** click the orb, ask "How many executives are posted in Delhi?" (or another question), then stay silent. ✅ when Yukti speaks the whole answer without stopping. Repeat with 5 different questions at normal speaker volume.
2. **Typed answers (×5):** type 5 questions in the ask bar. ✅ each answer is spoken to the end, and the chat shows **exactly one** agent bubble per question (no extra fragment bubble or repeated text).
3. **Tap to stop (×5):** while Yukti is speaking, alternately click the orb, click the mic button (it shows a ■ square while she speaks), and press Space. ✅ speech stops within about 0.3 s, the orb shows listening, and you can ask the next question straight away.
4. **Voice barge-in (×3):** while Yukti speaks, say clearly and a little louder than her, "Yukti, stop, show me Mumbai instead". ✅ she stops within about 0.5 s and answers the new question. Coughs, short "hmm", or her own voice should **not** stop her.
5. **Voice turns (×5):** ask 5 questions by voice. ✅ one question bubble and one answer bubble each; no "You interrupted before Yukti finished answering" unless you actually interrupted.
Report any failure with the step number, the question, and roughly what you heard. Backend log: `scratch/backend.log` in the agent's scratch dir (frames dropped by the gate are counted per session).
