## 4. Gap List & Boundaries

1. **FY23 Crude Realisation Narrative Range:** In `ONGC_AR_FY2022-23.txt` p.173 line 8089, crude net realization is printed as a narrative range ("between USD 70 and USD 75 per barrel") rather than an audited single point estimate. In strict adherence to `BUILD.md` §4.1/§4.3, no synthetic midpoint (e.g. 72.50) is created; the range is disclosed as an unprinted point estimate in the gap log.
2. **FY20 Standalone Statements:** ONGC did not publish a searchable digital text edition for FY20 (image-only scan in Piper depot). Per SPEC-01 §14 Q2, FY20 statements are provided by XBRL (₹1 cr precision) + the 10-year summary table; detailed note breakdowns for FY20 are left out rather than invented.
3. **Pre-FY24 Production Tables in ONGC\|JV layout:** The operations table with side-by-side `ONGC | JV share` columns is printed in AR FY26 p.55 for FY24–FY26, and in AR FY25 p.14 Operational Highlights for FY21–FY25. For FY13–FY20, quantity sold (`QTY.*`, `basis='sold'`) is loaded from the 10-year table.
4. **Overseas Hydrocarbon Assets (OVL):** All domestic nomination and domestic JV segments reconcile to ONGC standalone AR. Overseas reserves and production sit in OVL subsidiary accounts; consolidated segment note 50 (FY26) discloses `OUTSIDE_INDIA_EP` revenue (₹84,429.23 mn) and result (₹22,362.86 mn), which are captured in `fact_segment` under `scope='consolidated'`.
