## 4. Gap List & Boundaries

1. **FY20 Standalone Statements:** ONGC did not publish a searchable digital text edition for FY20 (image-only scan in Piper depot). Per SPEC-01 §14 Q2, FY20 statements are provided by XBRL (₹1 cr precision) + the 10-year summary table; detailed note breakdowns for FY20 are left out rather than invented.
2. **Pre-FY24 Production Tables in ONGC\|JV layout:** The operations table with side-by-side `ONGC | JV share` columns is printed in AR FY26 p.55 for FY24–FY26, and in AR FY25 p.14 Operational Highlights for FY21–FY25. For FY13–FY20, quantity sold (`QTY.*`, `basis='sold'`) is loaded from the 10-year table.
3. **Overseas Hydrocarbon Assets (OVL):** All domestic nomination and domestic JV segments reconcile to ONGC standalone AR. Overseas reserves and production sit in OVL subsidiary accounts; consolidated segment note 50 (FY26) discloses `OUTSIDE_INDIA_EP` revenue (₹84,429.23 mn) and result (₹22,362.86 mn), which are captured in `fact_segment` under `scope='consolidated'`.
4. **Gas Price Realisation (FY23 and earlier):** Detailed APM vs New Well gas realization splits began publishing in FY25 results press releases. For FY23, the Directors' Report / MD&A provides the domestic gas price revision range (H1: \$6.10/mmbtu, H2: \$8.57/mmbtu), which is captured with `notes` explaining the period pricing.
