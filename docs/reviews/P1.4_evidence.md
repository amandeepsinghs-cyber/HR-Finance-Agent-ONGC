## 5. Filing defects handled (deterministic rules, in the module docstring)
1. **Context dates are unreliable.** The FY18–FY21 files declare no `FourD`/`OneD`/`OneI` context element at all (948 facts noted `context_not_declared_in_file`). The FY23 and FY24 files declare `FourD` as a 3-month period, although its values are full-year. So `period_type` comes from the context **name**. The declared dates are kept in `context_period_declared`, with the note `context_period_mislabelled`.
2. **The FY21 cash flow is filed under `OneD`** (91 facts; standalone CFO ₹27,665.11 cr). Reg-33 cash flow is annual-only, so cash-flow facts get `period_type='annual'` and the note `cf_annual_filed_under_OneD`.
3. **`CashAndCashEquivalentsCashFlowStatement` appears twice per context.** The first is the opening balance and the second is the closing balance (`CF.CCE_OPEN` / `CF.CCE_CLOSE`). Check: the FY24 S closing balance of 34.55 ₹ cr = AR FY24 345.48 ₹ mn. The opening values look inconsistent: FY23 S opening is 0.00, and FY24 S opening is 77.19 vs FY23 S closing 27.08. These are not used, because AR is primary.
4. **Segment members** come from the `DescriptionOfReportableSegment`/`DescriptionOfOtherExpenses` facts (e.g. `a) Offshore`). Instant facts (`…01I`) use their `…01D` twin.
