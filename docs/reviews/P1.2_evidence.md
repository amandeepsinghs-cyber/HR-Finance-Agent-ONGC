**Pairs excluded on purpose** (they would otherwise appear as false failures):
- **Definition break:** AR FY26 "Total Expenses" excludes DD&A, but AR FY24/FY22 "Total Expenses" includes it. `test_total_expenses_definition_break` shows AR26 TE + DD&A = AR24 TE to the ₹ mn for FY17–FY23 (FY24: +427, the reclassification above). The AR26 key is therefore `total expenses excl dda`.
- **Generic child labels** (`Others`, `Current`, `Non Current`, `Revenue`, `Capital`) are keyed with their parent. AR26 "Work in Progress: / Others" is not AR24 product-sales "Others".

## 5. Deviations / open points (for R-O review)

1. **Side-tab rule:** the packet says to drop text right of column 165. That is not used, because AR FY26 PDF p.24 data runs to column 169 and a cut would lose the FY17 column. The parser instead takes the last N numeric tokens, drops trailing words after the last value, and skips no-number lines that start right of column 120. Same intent, no data loss.
2. **Extra row keys** beyond §5.3 + 4: `report_vintage`, `is_nil`, `source_page_label` (SPEC-01 §4.1 folio) and `notes` (`aligned_by_position` for rows with fewer than N cells: AR24/AR22 "Road and Infrastructure Cess", "Expenses relating to buyback…").
3. **Table extent:** AR FY24 and AR FY22 also include the "Statement of Income and Retained Earnings" pages (same 10 FY header). The "Statement of Financial Position" pages ("As at March 31" 3-line header, 9 columns + a separate FY15 page) are **not** parsed. They are out of the 10-yr-row rule set; P1.3 / a follow-up can add them. Page ranges are fixed per edition in `EDITIONS` (AR26 2 pages, AR24 4, AR22 4).
4. **Label-continuation guard:** a no-number line after a data line is a heading (not a continuation) when the next line is a `-` child. Example: AR24 "Exploration Costs written off" followed by "-Survey Costs".
5. **Label truncation:** AR FY22 "Road and Infrastructure" loses "Cess", which wraps onto the next page. It is aliased in cross-check.
6. **Malformed token:** AR FY24 `17,988)` (OCI FY19, missing "(") parses as −17,988, which matches AR FY26 `(17,988)`.
7. `uv.lock` is committed for reproducible `uv sync`; it is not in the §3 file list.
8. **AR FY24/FY22 block labels:** these editions have no "Financial Strength" heading, so equity/borrowings rows there carry `block=financial_value`. Mapping to `line_code` (P1.6) should not rely on `block`.
9. **Natural gas units differ by edition:** AR FY24/FY22 report natural gas in MMM3 (`unit=mn_m3`, e.g. 15,927), while AR FY26 reports BCM (15.927). They are not paired in cross-check because the labels and units differ.
