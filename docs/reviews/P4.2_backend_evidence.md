## 1. Files Changed (P4.2 Backend Scope)

1. `yukti/contracts/answer_contract.schema.json`
   - Added `"statement"`, `"sensitivity_grid"`, `"tornado"` to `visual.properties.type.enum` (SPEC-03 §5.1).
   - Added `"statement": {"$ref": "#/$defs/statement_spec"}` to `visual.properties`.
   - Added `statement_row`, `statement_section`, and `statement_spec` definitions under `$defs` without bumping `contract_version` (`enum: [1, 2]`).
2. `yukti/backend/app/brain/statement_builder.py` (new)
   - `v3_field_enabled(token="statement")` checking `YUKTI_CONTRACT_V3_FIELDS`.
   - `clean_statement_label(raw_label, line_code)` stripping artificial numbering/indent prefixes (`"A1."`, `"I."`, `"   — "`, `"Less: "`, `"Add/(Less): "`, `"Memo: "`) and formula suffixes (`"(I + II)"`, `"(A1 to A7)"`) (BUG-39).
   - `STATEMENT_LINE_META` + `_merge_contract_line_classes()` covering SPEC-01 SSOT (`PL.*`, `BS.*`, `CF.*`), short (`REV_OPS`, etc.), and 10-yr snapshot (`gross_revenue_inr_cr`, `oil_and_gas_assets_inr_cr`, `operating_cash_flow_inr_cr`, etc.) codes across P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow.
   - `build_statement_spec(...)` filtering to `verified=TRUE` rows only, omitting any requested period that lacks verified rows with a caveat footnote, preserving `None`/`null` (never coercing to `0`), preserving `100%` of SSOT `line_order` (`order`), computing `yoy_pct` (omitted for `subtotal`/`total` rows whose base `<= 0` per SPEC-09 §4.1), and computing `totals_check` (`identity`, `passed`, `max_abs_delta_cr`) for P&L, Balance Sheet (`A = E + L`), and Cash Flow (`Closing Cash = Opening Cash + Net Cash Change`).
3. `yukti/backend/app/brain/presenter.py` & `yukti/backend/app/turn_runner.py`
   - `build_visual` accepts optional `periods: Optional[List[str]] = None` and constructs `stmt_spec = build_statement_spec(...)` across `pnl_statement`, `balance_sheet`, and `cash_flow_statement`.
   - When `v3_field_enabled("statement")` is `True`, sets `vis["statement"] = stmt_spec`, caps `vis["kpis"]` at 3 (`SPEC-09 §4.2 S10`), and sets `vis["type"] = "statement"` when no non-table chart kind was explicitly requested (or on `BridgeError`).
4. `yukti/contracts/fixtures/statement/{pnl,bs,cf}_synthetic.json` (new)
   - `pnl_synthetic.json`: 5-year (`FY26..FY22`) standalone P&L AnswerContract v2 fixture (`sections: [income, expenses, profit]`, `PAT` `kind: "total"`, `totals_check.passed: true`, `synthetic: true`).
   - `bs_synthetic.json`: 5-year (`FY26..FY22`) standalone Balance Sheet AnswerContract v2 fixture (`totals_check: {"identity": "A = E + L", "passed": true, "max_abs_delta_cr": 0.0}`, `synthetic: true`).
   - `cf_synthetic.json`: 5-year (`FY26..FY22`) standalone Cash Flow AnswerContract v2 fixture (`totals_check: {"identity": "Closing Cash = Opening Cash + Net Cash Change", "passed": true, "max_abs_delta_cr": 0.0}`, `synthetic: true`).
5. `yukti/backend/tests/test_statement_builder.py` (new)
   - 15 unit and integration tests covering schema validation, SSOT `line_order` preservation, label cleaning, `verified=TRUE` filter + omitted period caveat + `None` preservation, `yoy_pct` base<=0 omission, `totals_check` pass/fail, `bridge()` consumption of `StatementSpec` across all 5 periods, and `YUKTI_CONTRACT_V3_FIELDS=statement` presenter flag gating.
6. `yukti/backend/tests/test_contract_v2_fixtures.py`
   - Extended schema and contract v2 invariant checks to `yukti/contracts/fixtures/statement/*.json`.
