Nothing is plugged or interpolated.
- **Gate `W4` Kd-Sanity Flags:** The sanity band in [`build_wacc.py:64-70`](../../financial_ssot/wacc/build_wacc.py#L64-L70) is strictly `[rf_used_pct - 1.0, rf_used_pct + 3.0]` as specified in SPEC-02 §3.1/§3.4 (not widened).
  - **`FY24`** (`kd_pretax = 7.2861%` vs `Rf = 7.0667%`, band `[6.0667%, 10.0667%]`) passes all gates (`verified = True`, `failed_rule_ids = []`, `wacc_pct = 13.0744%`).
  - **`FY17`** (`21.3465%`, zero borrowings and `₹41.796 cr` finance lease obligations), **`FY18`** (`2.5737%`, year-end `₹25,592 cr` HPCL acquisition borrowing jump with ~2 months of interest in P&L against a 2-point opening/closing mean debt), and **`FY19, FY21–FY23, FY25–FY26`** (`3.6890%–5.5879%`, where ~60–77% of standalone debt is USD-denominated offshore rig/vessel lease liabilities discounted at USD incremental borrowing rates plus short-term/foreign-currency debt, matching SPEC-02 §4's worked check `FY26 Kd ≈ 4.13%`) fall outside `[Rf_INR - 1.0pp, Rf_INR + 3.0pp]`.
  - Rather than inventing an unsourced AAA rating spread (no CRISIL/ICRA spread seed is on disk) or widening the band to force `verified=True`, `build_wacc.py` keeps the true statement-derived `kd_pretax_pct` as `kd_used_pct`, sets `kd_flag = 'out_of_band'`, appends `'W4'` to `failed_rule_ids`, sets `verified = False`, and records `"Kd out of sanity band [Rf - 1pp, Rf + 3pp]"` in `notes`. This is completely transparent and faithful to Truth Rule `D-35`.
