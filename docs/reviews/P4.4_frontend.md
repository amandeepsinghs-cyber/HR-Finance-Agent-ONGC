## 4. Findings (Round 1 — Resolved in `cb0a01b`)

| # | Severity | File:line | Finding | Resolution (`cb0a01b`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M1** | **Blocking (Resolved)** | `yukti/frontend/src/charts/compat.ts:347-352` | Re-plotting a single-row visual with a `target` column to `'gauge'` (or `'bullet'`) stripped the `target` column via `pivotSingleRow` and rendered `form: 'kpi'`. | Fixed (`compat.ts:350-352`): `applied === 'gauge' \|\| applied === 'bullet'` preserves `data = p.rows` including its `target` column; covered by `M1` test in `test_portfolio.mjs`. |
| **M2** | **Blocking (Resolved)** | `yukti/frontend/src/charts/compat.ts:175-180, 353-367` | (a) `replot(records, 'box')` replaced records with 1-row-per-group `countBy` headcounts; (b) `compatibility(p, 'box')` rejected grouped-record datasets where `p.dimKey` had repeated group values (like `box_tenure.json`). | Fixed (`compat.ts:178-180, 353-357`): `repeated = new Set(p.rows.map(r => r[p.dimKey])).size < p.n` passes the `box` gate, and `applied === 'box'` keeps `data = p.rows`, `x = p.shape === 'records' && p.groupable.length ? p.groupable[0] : p.dimKey`, `y = p.primary`; covered by `M2` test in `test_portfolio.mjs`. |
| **M3** | **Blocking (Resolved)** | `yukti/frontend/src/charts/compat.ts:170-174, 353-367` | `replot(records, 'heatmap')` collapsed `data` to 1 category via `countBy`, causing `planChart` to fall back to `form = 'column'`. | Fixed (`compat.ts:171-174, 358-372`): `records → heatmap` cross-tabulates `p.groupable[0] × p.groupable[1]` into `{ [g1]: a, [g2]: b, count: v }` so `planChart` renders `form: 'heatmap'`; covered by `M3` test in `test_portfolio.mjs`. |
| **S1** | Non-blocking (Resolved) | `yukti/frontend/src/charts/options.ts:1520-1524` | `boxOption` re-ran `fiveNumber(inside)` after filtering outliers, shifting `q1`, `median`, and `q3`. | Fixed (`options.ts:1520-1526`): keeps true `[q1, md, q3]` of all values with whiskers `[Math.min(...body), q1, md, q3, Math.max(...body)]`; covered by `S1` test in `test_portfolio.mjs`. |
