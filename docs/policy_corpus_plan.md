# ONGC HR & Finance Policy Corpus: What We Can Source

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Conclusion:** About 20 documents can be downloaded legally and directly: ONGC's governance policies plus Government of India rules and acts. The core HR rulebooks are internal to ONGC. These are the transfer policy (JRTP), the Book of Delegated Powers, Leave, Conduct/Discipline/Appeal (CDA), travel allowance (TA/DA) and Medical rules. **Recommendation:** for the demo, write "demo-faithful" synthetic versions of the internal rulebooks, modelled on publicly known rules. Do **not** ingest leaked copies from Scribd. Building on leaked copies would be a credibility and compliance risk with ONGC itself.
