## 3. Exact requirements
1. **Hindi narration (`BUG-19, BUG-46`, `SPEC-11 §6.2`):** When `language == "hi"` (or Devanagari/Hindi query detected), `narrator.py` / `presenter.py` produces a clean Hindi `spoken_summary` and `display_text` using Indian numbering (`लाख`, `करोड़`), never concatenating English and Hindi in one bubble (`BEH-07.11, BEH-07.35`).
2. **Coverage claim guard (`SPEC-11 §5`):** `narrator.py` rejects any sentence matching `\b(only|up to|until|not available|don'?t have)\b.*\b(FY|20\d\d|data)\b` unless generated from `CoverageManifest` (`grounding_drop: coverage_claim`).
3. **Voice verbatim & dedupe (`SPEC-11 §6.1, §6.3`, `BUG-17, 18`):**
   - Each spoken output carries a unique `speak_id`.
   - When a contract exists for `turn_id`, bubble text is `display_text`; Live transcript chunks for that turn or after `turn_complete` for a closed `speak_id` never spawn duplicate or fragment bubbles.
   - Partial voice utterances `< 3` words ending in a function word (`"is the policy about"`) are held/ignored rather than submitted as fragment turns.
4. **Voice spike decision (`SPEC-11 §10 AC-03`):** Record the 20-turn EN/HI verbatim evaluation and option decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
5. **Feedback regressions (`BEH-07.30`–`BEH-07.35`):** Encode all 6 multi-turn feedback regressions in `tests/test_p52_voice_hindi_beh07.py` and verify 6/6 pass on 3 consecutive runs.
