## 5. Exact requirements
1. `statements.parse(text_path, report_vintage, scope) -> list[dict]` (bronze schema + `fiscal_year`, `period_type`, `statement`, `note_ref`, `roman_ref`, `column_index`). Columns: `Particulars | Note | CY | PY` (+ a 1-Apr opening column when 3 values are present → `period_type='opening'`).
2. Each line yields one row **per numeric column**; `period_end` from the header ("Year Ended March 31, 2026" / "As at March 31, 2026").
3. Roman-numeral lines (`III Total income (I+II)`) keep `roman_ref` and their formula text in `raw_label`.
4. Multi-line labels (e.g. "Changes in inventories of finished/ semi-finished goods and work in" + "progress") are joined (P1.2 continuation rule).
5. Remove side-tab noise (column > 165 or known tab words).
6. `notes.parse(text_path, report_vintage)` returns rows for the notes in §2, with `source_table='note_<n>'`.
7. Self-validation inside the parser output (flags only, never altering values): `pnl_chain_ok` (PBT − tax = profit for the year ±0.01 ₹ mn), `bs_identity_ok` (total assets = total equity + total liabilities ±0.01), `cash_tie_ok` (CF closing C&CE = BS C&CE ±0.01). Pages failing any flag are listed in `financial_ssot/out/statements_parse_issues.csv` for O's Gemini fallback.
8. CLI: `python -m financial_ssot.extract.statements --out financial_ssot/out/statements_raw.parquet` covering FY17, FY18, FY19 (own ARs) and FY24, FY25, FY26 (own ARs, with comparatives FY16, FY23 etc.), standalone + consolidated. AR FY22/FY23: parse too if the headers are found (comparative FY21/FY22).
