## 3. Exact requirements
1. `CoverageManifest` schema (`SPEC-11 §5`):
   - `{generated_at, source: "bq"|"snapshot", finance: [{scope, statement, fy_min, fy_max, line_count, source_systems}], market: {yahoo_available, last_price_date}, wacc: {fy_min, fy_max}, hr: [{table, grain, as_of, rows, synthetic: true}], field: {...}, documents: [{type, fy_list, count}], policies: {count, illustrative_count}}`.
   - Built from verified SSOT parquet/snapshot and cached for 15 minutes.
2. `app/intents/coverage.py`:
   - Matches coverage queries (`what (data|information) (do you|you) have|which years|data (available|coverage)|kya data hai|kitne saal ka data|do you have .* (balance sheet|income statement|cash flow|data) for|when you say upto 2026`).
   - For general coverage (`F47`): returns `visual.type = "table"` listing domains, scopes, years (`FY17–FY26` statements, `FY13–FY26` 10-yr summary, `FY17–FY26` WACC, Employee-360 as of `2026-10-19`, Field Economics `FY17–FY26`, Policy corpus) + crisp `spoken_summary` (EN or HI).
   - For specific availability (`F48`, e.g. `"Do you have the balance sheet for 2024?"` or `"when you say upto 2026, 2024 is before 2026?"`): answers `"Yes, FY 2023-24 (FY24) is available in the verified Annual Report dataset (FY17–FY26)..."` and includes a drilldown/follow-up chip to open the FY24 statement immediately.
3. Works with LLM completely disabled (`AC-02`: mock LLM raises `RuntimeError`, F47 and F48 still pass 100%).
