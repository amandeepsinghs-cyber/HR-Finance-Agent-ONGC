## 5. Exact requirements
1. Per FY (FY17…FY26): inputs exactly as SPEC-02 §3.1 (`rf_gsec_10y`, `default_spread_india`, `rf_used`, `erp_mature`, `crp_india`, `erp_total_india`, `beta_2y_weekly_raw/adj`, `beta_5y_monthly_raw/adj`, `beta_industry_relevered` when prices are unavailable, `ke`, `kd_pretax`, `kd_used`, `tax_rate`, `equity_mv`, `debt_bv`, `wacc`). **`rf_used = rf_gsec_10y` (raw INR 10-yr G-sec at FY-end; `rf_method='gsec_raw'`).** `default_spread_india` is stored only to compute the sensitivity column `wacc_alt_gsec_less_spread_pct` (Rf = G-sec − spread); it never feeds `wacc`.
