# ONGC HR & Finance — Public Policy Source Pack (MANIFEST)

Collected: 19 Oct 2026 (UTC) for the ONGC CMD HR & Finance demo agent.
Layout: `raw/` = original files exactly as downloaded (PDF or saved HTML); `text/` = extracted text (`pdftotext -layout`, or **OCR via RapidOCR** for scanned PDFs, noted below; judgments are cleaned HTML→Markdown).
All files were checked after download (real PDF/HTML, page counts confirmed, no HTML error pages).

**source_type legend:** `official_ongc` (ongcindia.com) · `official_govt` (gov.in / nic.in / sansad.in / a CPSE's official site hosting a govt order) · `court_judgment` (indiankanoon.org copy) · `parliament` · `association` (ex-ONGC executives' association, exongcexecutives.org, posting ONGC circulars) · `other`.
**Confidence:** High = official issuer, authentic full text · Medium = authentic but older/partial/OCR'd, or mirrored by a third party · Low = authenticity or currency uncertain.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Biggest finds:** (1) the full **ONGC Conduct, Discipline & Appeal Rules, 1994 (amended 2011)**, 103 pages, published by ONGC itself on its RTI page (P1-01); (2) the full **ONGC Travelling Allowance Regulations (updated 2016)**, 54 pages, an authentic ONGC Corporate Policy document posted by the ex-executives association (P1-16). The ONGC RTI Section 4(1)(b) page (P1-02) also gives the official **list** of ONGC's internal HR rulebooks (Leave Rules 1995, Service Rules 1995, TA Regulations, R&P Regulations 1980, JRTP, PRBS, and others).
