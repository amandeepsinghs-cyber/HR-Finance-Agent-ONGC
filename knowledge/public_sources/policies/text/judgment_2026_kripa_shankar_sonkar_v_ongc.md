 Information sought

:

1.    The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 08-05-2025 seeking the
following information:

             "1. Attested photocopy of approval by which the medical benefits in
             favour of myself, spouse and disabled son was accorded in the
             month of june 2020 at the time of my superannuation.

             2. Attested photocopies of circulars pertaining to availing of medical
             benefits w.e.f January 2008 to June 30th 2020.

             3. Medical benefits suspended infavour of myself, spouse and
             disabled son since January 2025 by ONGC, "Seperated Employees
             Cell." In connection with this, attested photocopy of order/approval
             by which the medical benefits had been suspended. The expenses of
             photocopies shall be paid by me on acknowledging of the
             communication pertaining to expenses of photocopies. Since the
             aforementioned information pertains to myself, hence, the same
             should be divulged to me within stipulated period."

 2.   Having not received any response from the CPIO, the Appellant filed a
 First Appeal on 20-06-2025, FAA's order, which is not adjudicated as per the
 record of the Commission.

3.    Feeling aggrieved and dissatisfied, Appellant approached the Commission
with the instant Second Appeal.

Relevant Facts emerged during Hearing:

The following were present:-

Appellant: Absent.

 Respondent: Ms. Seema Milred Bara, GM (HR) & CPIO, attended the hearing in
person.

4.    The Respondent submitted, that the appellant sought attested photocopy
of approval by which the medical benefits in favour of the appellant, his spouse
and disabled son were accorded in the month of June 2020, at the time of his
superannuation; attested photocopy of order/approval by which the medical
benefits had been suspended etc. The respondent also submitted, that they
had provided point-wise reply to the appellant vide letter dated 06.10.2025.

5.    The Commission queried the respondent, regarding delay of almost 4
months in replying to the appellant. To this, the respondent submitted, that it
took time to collect information from the custodian of the information. The
Commission further queried the respondent, regarding the irrelevant replies in
letter dated 06.10.2025. To this, the respondent did not give any satisfactory
response. The Commission also queried the respondent, as to whether their
FAA had replied to the 1st appeal filed by the appellant. To this, the respondent
submitted, that there is no such FAA order on record. The respondent further
submitted, that the appellant was on deputation, and had less than 15 years of
service. Hence, the medical benefits were suspended.

6.    Written submission dated 01.06.2026, letter dated 06.10.2025 and order
dated 13.11.2025 issued by the Hon'ble Delhi High Court, filed by the
respondent are taken on record and relevant portion of the same is reproduced
below:

              "1. Approval was obtained vide note dated 01.11.2022 and
              09.07.2020 for extension of medical facilities for Mr. Kripa Shankar
              Sonkar, spouse and disabled son as per existing ONGC Medical
              rules.

              3.    As per directions of Corporate Policy, post-retirement medical

Interim Decision:

7.    The Commission, after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the
case, hearing the respondent and perusal of the records, observes that the
respondent did not give an appropriate reply to the appellant vide letter dated
06.10.2025. The Commission finds, that the appellant sought photocopy of the
approval by which the medical benefits in favour of the appellant, his spouse
and disabled son were accorded in the month of June 2020, at the time of his
superannuation. But the respondent had provided approvals obtained vide
notes dated 01.11.2022 and 09.07.2020. Also, the respondent had not provided
the sought circular against point no.2. In view of the above, the respondent is
directed to provide a revised point-wise reply, incorporating the following
sought information: point no.1 - attested photocopy of approval by which the
medical benefits in favour of appellant, his spouse and disabled son was
accorded in the month of June 2020, at the time of his superannuation; point
no.2 - circulars pertaining to availing of medical benefits w.e.f January 2008 to
June 30th 2020; point no.3 - photocopy of order/approval by which the medical
benefits had been suspended.

8.    The respondent CPIO is directed to provide all the sought certified
copies/attested copies in the aforesaid revised point-wise reply, by endorsing

 the documents as per paragraph no.2 of the Office Memorandum dated
06.10.2015 issued by the Department of Personnel & Training. The respondent
is also directed to redact third-party information such as officers' names,
identity etc. in consonance with Section 10 of the RTI Act, 2005, at relevant
documents/note sheets/communications, while providing the information.

9.    The respondent is directed to provide the aforesaid revised point-wise
reply to the appellant, within 15 days from the date of receipt of this order,
under intimation to the Commission - both through post and via uploading on
http://dsscic.nic.in/online-link-paper-compliance/add.

10. Apart from the above, the Commission finds, that the respondent has
provided incomplete information to the appellant after a delay of around 04
months. In view of the above, the then incumbent CPIO is hereby SHOW-
CAUSED as to why penalty U/s 20(1) of the RTI Act, 2005 should not be imposed
upon him/her for the aforesaid reasons. The then incumbent CPIO is directed
to submit detailed written explanation to the Commission, for providing
incomplete information to the appellant, and for the delay - both through post
and via uploading on http://dsscic.nic.in/online-link-paper-compliance/add,
within 15 days from the date of receipt of this order. Accordingly, the appeal is
reserved for further orders.
