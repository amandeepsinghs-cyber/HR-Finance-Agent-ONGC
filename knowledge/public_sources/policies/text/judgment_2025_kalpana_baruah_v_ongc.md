                                          ORDER

Date : 10-11-2025

      Heard Mr. P.K. Gogoi, learned counsel for the applicant. Also heard Mr. T. Das,
learned counsel appearing for the respondents No. 1, 2, 4 to 7.

      This is an application praying for amendment of the connected writ petition
particularly the address of the respondent No.6.

                                                                                   Page No.# 3/3

       The    applicant/petitioner   has   filed   the    connected   writ   petition   seeking
release/withdrawal of her Post Retirement Benefit Scheme (PRBS) amount along with
interest thereof.

       It is contended by the learned counsel for the petitioner that in the connected writ
petition wrong address has been given in respect of the respondent No.6 inadvertently,
therefore, the correct address of the respondent may be allowed to be given by way of
amendment.

       Learned counsel for the respondents submits that he does not have any objection to
the aforesaid prayer.

       Having considered that the petitioner appears to have given wrong address in respect
of the respondent No.6 in the connected WP(C) 1429/2025, I am of the considered view
that the present application is deserved to be allowed.

       Accordingly, the applicant is allowed to amend the cause title particularly in respect
of the respondent No.6 as under:-

              "6. The Group General Manager, (H.R. Department) Oil and Natural Gas
              Corporation Ltd., Assam Assets Nazira, Dist-Sivasagar, Assam, Pin- 7856885"

    I.A stands allowed and disposed of.
