                                      OFFICE ORDER ( 33 /2022)


Subject: Extension of Medical Facilities to Dependent Parent(s) of Retired Employees


    Executive Committee (EC) in its 574th meeting held on 18th July, 2022 at New Delhi has
accorded approval to the following:


(i)     ONGC will extend medical facilities to the dependent parent(s) of the retired employees
       under Post-Retirement Medical Benefit Scheme. This will cover dependent parent(s) of
       already retired employees also. Only such dependent parent(s) shall be considered who were
       availing medical facilities from ONGC at the time of retirement of the employee.


(ii)    Employees willing to avail such facility, shall contribute 50°/o of last drawn basic pay at the
       time of retirement per beneficiary for availing medical facility for dependent parent(s).


(iii) Employees already retired, who wish to avail medical facility for their dependent parent(s)
     shall also make the same contribution @ 50% of last drawn basic pay per beneficiaryo They
       may contact I/C SEE of the respective location for the same.


(iv) One time option would be given to the retired employees to avail / join the scheme within a
       period of 6 months from the date of issue of Office Order and the employees who would be
       retiring after the date of office order will have to exercise the option to avail /join the scheme
       before retirement,
