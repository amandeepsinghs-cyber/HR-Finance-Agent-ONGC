                          MEDICAL EXAMINATION OF EMPLOYEES RULES, 2026


1.    SHORT TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT

      These rules may be called the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited Medical
      Examination of Employees Rules, 2026. They shall come into force w.e.f. date of
      issue of orders.

2.    APPLICABILITY

     2.1          Every candidate/fresh entrant including those appointed on contract, part
                 time or tenure basis in order to be eligible for appointment in the service of
                 the Company will be required to undergo a medical examination of the
                 appropriate standard by a Medical Authority as mentioned in Sub-Rule 3(e)
                 and will be required to produce a certificate of fitness from that Authority.

     2.2          An employee of the Company may be subjected to medical examination
                 under these Rules with the approval of the Appointing Authority as specified
                 in Rule 6.

     2.3         Provided further that the following categories of persons/employees will not
                 be required to undergo medical examination for appointment in the service
                 of the Company:

            i.      An employee of the Company, who has been medically examined by
                    the appropriate Medical Authority at the time of his initial appointment,
                    when appointed/promoted to another post in the Company requiring
                    medical examination of equivalent standard.
            ii.     An employee of the Central Government/State Government/Public
                    Sector Undertaking joining the service of the Company without a break
                    in service.
           iii.       An employee of the Central Government/State Government/Public
                     Sector Undertaking appointed on deputation basis in the Company and
                     is subsequently absorbed.
           iv.        A person specifically appointed for a period not exceeding 3 months
                     unless it is decided to extend the appointment beyond 3 months. (if
                     beyond three months, Pre Employment Medical Examination need to be
                     done as per ONGC guidelines)
           v.       A retrenched employee of the Company/State Government/Public
                    Sector Undertaking appointed in the Company within one year of
                    his/her retrenchment, provided he/she had undergone medical
                    examination of the standard applicable to the post, in which he/she is
                    re-employed prior to his/her retrenchment.
                 NOTE:
                     The above exemptions will not apply in a case, where a fresh medical
                     examination in respect of all the candidates is prescribed under these
                     rules.
                                                                                          Page 2 of 32
3.    DEFINITIONS

      For the purpose of these Rules unless the context otherwise requires:

     a. "Appointing Authority" in relation to any post means the Chairman/Board of
        Directors and includes any person or authority to whom the power to make
        appointment to that post has been so delegated;
     b. "Board" means, the Board of Directors of the Company and includes in
        relation to the exercise of powers, any Committee of the Board or any officer
        of the Company to whom the Board delegates any of its powers.
     c. "Company" means the Oil and Natural                    Gas   Corporation   Limited,
        incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956.
     d. "Employee" means a person who is employed in the Company in a
        regular scale of pay and includes any such person, whose services are
        temporarily placed at the disposal of State Government or the Central
        Government or any Central/State Government Undertaking.
     e. "Medical Authority" means the ONGC Medical Officer nominated by the Head
        of Medical Services, who conducts the medical examination in accordance
        with the approved guidelines on the subject matter.
          NOTE:
          For women candidates, a female medical officer will conduct the
          examination. If a female medical officer is not available at the work center,
          the examination may be conducted by a male medical officer in the
          presence of a female nurse or attendant.
     f.   “Medical Fee” means the expenditure incurred on account of investigations &
          consultations done during Pre-Employment Medical Examination of the
          candidates.
     g. "Prescribed Medical Qualifications" means the medical qualifications
        included in one of the first, second or part-II of the third Schedule of the Indian
        Medical Council Act, 1956.
     h.   In these Rules, Masculine refers to Feminine also.

4. MEDICAL EXAMINATION AT THE TIME OF FIRST APPOINTMENT

 4.1       The offer of appointment will include set of physical standards required for
           the post as given in Annexure ONGC: MER-V.
 4.2        Once a candidate is asked by the competent authority to produce a
           medical certificate of fitness either for entry into the Company’s service or
           for any other purpose and has actually been examined and declared unfit,
           it is not open to such authority to use its discretion to ignore the certificate
           that has been produced. In a case, where it becomes absolutely necessary
           to retain the services of such an employee on a temporary basis due to
           certain administrative reasons, it should be referred to the Appointing
           Authority for approval.
 4.3       The certificate of medical fitness shall be sent by the Medical Officer direct
           to the Appointing Authority concerned in the proforma prescribed at


                                                                                      Page 3 of 32
       Annexures ONGC: MER-I, ONGC: MER-II, ONGC: MER-III and in the case of
       temporary unfitness, the Medical Authority shall inform the candidate as well
       as the Appointing Authority, as per proforma at Annexure ONGC: MER-IV.
 4.4     If the Medical Certificate, even if it declares the candidate fit for service,
       discloses that the candidate had at an earlier stage suffered from a disease,
       the recurrence of which in future may incapacitate the candidate for long
       durations, the Appointing Authority may, at its discretion, obtain a second
       Medical opinion from a Specialist in the concerned disease before offering
       the appointment and in such a case, the second medical opinion shall be
       deemed to supersede the recommendation made by the Medical
       Authority.

 NOTE:
 The cost of medical fee in respect of the second medical opinion shall be
 reimbursed by the Company at the rates prescribed by the Company from time to
 time, as well as the expenditure incurred on Pre-Employment medical examination
 excluding traveling/lodging/boarding expenditures.
 The second medical opinion should be obtained from an empanelled hospital
 referred by ONGC doctor.
 4.5   The medical fee for the medical examination under this Rule shall be borne
       by the Company. In case the Medical fee is paid by the candidate initially,
       the same shall be reimbursable to him/her at the rates prescribed by the
       Company from time to time.
 4.6   No travelling allowance and boarding/lodging charges will, however, be
       admissible for undertaking a journey to obtain the medical certificate under
       this Rule from the Medical Authority.

5. STANDARD OF PHYSICAL FITNESS

 5.1   The standards of physical fitness for posts are given in Annexure-ONGC: MER-
       V.

       Provided that:

       Stammering shall not be considered a physical defect requiring
       disqualification of a candidate for a clerical post.

 5.2   Relaxation:
         i)   For female candidates:
              A female candidate who as a result of tests is found to be pregnant
              of twelve weeks standing or below will not be declared temporarily
              unfit for appointment provided the post to which she is to be
              appointed does not carry hazardous nature of duties or does not
              prescribe elaborate training.

       ii)    For persons with disabilities:

       The persons with disabilities shall have to undergo usual medical
       examination. However, they shall not be subjected to tests related to the

        details mentioned in the disability certificate issued by the Competent
        Government Authority for persons with disabilities.


6. MEDICAL EXAMINATION FOR SUSPECTED DISEASES/DISABILITIES

  6.1    Where the Appointing Authority has reason to believe that an employee is
        suffering from a contagious disease or physical and/or mental disability
        which, in his opinion, interferes with the efficient discharge of his duties, or
        having attained/crossed the normal age of retirement is no longer able to
        maintain the required efficiency/ability that Authority may direct the
        employee to undergo a medical examination.

  6.2    In pursuance of the recommendations given by the Medical Authority, the
        Appointing Authority may require the employee either to proceed on leave
        or if he is already on leave to continue to remain on leave or to retire from
        service. The leave granted under this Rule shall be of such nature and for
        such period as would be admissible to the employee under the Rule if he
        had applied for the leave on medical certificate, provided that the period
        of leave shall not extend beyond the date of expiry of the period
        recommended by the Medical Authority.

  6.3   An employee declared by the examining Medical Authority to be
        permanently incapacitated for further service shall be retired from service.
        The authority which directed him/her to undergo the medical examination
        shall inform him/her in writing of the action proposed to be taken in regard
        to him/her indicating briefly the grounds on which such action is proposed
        to be taken.

  6.4   The employee shall also be informed that:-

        i)     Subject to the relevant provisions of ONGC Leave Rules, 1995 and any
              orders regarding grant of leave to persons suffering from specified
              diseases like tuberculosis, or any other active infectious diseases, his
              retirement will have effect on the expiry of a period of one month from
              the date of communication, unless he/she desires to retire from an
              earlier date.

        ii)    he/she may submit, if he/she so desires, within a period of one month, a
              request to be examined by Medical Review Board, supported by prima
              facie evidence that good grounds exist for doing so.

  6.5   For the period from the date of communication up to the date of retirement,
        the employee shall be granted leave under the rules applicable to his/her
        post or service as if he/she had applied for leave on medical certificate.

  6.6    On receipt of an application for review, the Appointing Authority shall take
        steps to constitute a Special Review Board. If the Review Board confirms the
        opinion of the examining medical authority, the retirement of the employee
        shall, subject to the relevant provisions of ONGC Leave Rules, 1995 be
        effective from the date on which the decision is communicated to the
        employee. If on the other hand, the Special Review Board recommends


                                                                                   Page 5 of 32
        grant of leave to the employee, action shall be taken as provided in sub-
        Rule 6.2 above.

  6.7   The medical fee, as well as charges incurred on cardiac investigations, X-ray
        examination, Medical Specialist’s fee, Hospitalization charges, laboratory
        fee etc. where such examinations or hospitalisation is considered necessary
        by the Medical Authority, for medical examination under this Rule, shall be
        borne by the Company. Where such fee is paid by the employee initially,
        the same shall be reimbursable to him/her.

7. MEDICAL EXAMINATION OF AN EMPLOYEE SENT ON DEPUTATION / TRAINING
ABROAD

An employee sent on deputation/training abroad at the expenses of the Company
may at the discretion of the Competent Authority be required to undergo a medical
examination by the Medical Authority for ascertaining whether he/she is physically fit
to undertake the journey as well as to withstand the rigours of climate of the
country/ countries he/she may visit during the course of his deputation/training.

8. MEDICAL CERTIFICATE FOR GRANT OF LEAVE

  8.1   Every application for leave or extension of leave on Medical grounds shall
        be accompanied by a Medical Certificate from Medical Authority.

  8.2    The authority competent to sanction leave may at its discretion, secure a
        second medical opinion wherever necessary. The cost on account of the
        medical fee and other charges payable to the Medical Officer/Medical
        Board constituting the second Medical opinion as per ONGC guidelines shall
        be borne by the Company in the same manner as specified under Rule 6.7.

  8.3    An employee who has been granted leave on Medical Certificate shall not
        be allowed to return to duty unless he/she produces a Medical Certificate of
        fitness from the same medical authority or an appropriate authority from the
        same hospital.

9. Communication

  9.1    In case where an employee or a candidate for appointment in Company’s
        service is declared unfit for retention in Company’s service or for
        appointment in Company’s service by a Medical Officer or by a Medical
        Board, as the case may be, the grounds for rejection may be
        communicated to him/her in broad terms without giving the minute details
        regarding the defects pointed out by the Medical Officer/Medical Board.

10. INTERPRETATION

In case of any doubt regarding any of the provisions of these Rules, the matter shall
be referred to the Chief, ER, who shall decide the same in consultation with
Corporate Finance.

Power to relax any of the provisions under these Rules shall vest in the Chairman of
the Company, who shall exercise such powers in consultation with Director (HR) and
Director (Finance).

12. POWER TO AMEND

The Company may from time to time amend, modify or add to these Rules and all
such amendments, modifications or additions shall take effect from the date stated
therein.

  13.1 The existing ONGC Medical Examination of Employees Rules, 1996 are
       hereby repealed.

  13.2 Nothing in these Rules or any repeal affected thereby shall affect or
       be deemed to affect anything done or action taken under the said
       ONGC Medical Examination of Employees Rules, 1996, before
       commencement                 of             these              Rules.

INSTRUCTIONS TO THE COMPETENT MEDICAL AUTHORITY REGARDING PHYSICAL EXAMINATION OF
CANDIDATES FOR APPOINTMENT IN OIL AND NATURAL GAS CORPORATION LIMITED.

  1.    Before taking up the Examination of a candidate, please verify that you are examining the same person whose
       photograph has been attested by the authorized officer of the HR Department who has directed the candidate to you
       for the medical examination.
       It is absolutely essential to extend due regard to candidates privacy and decency. Medical Examination should be
       done thoroughly and if a candidate does not submit to this even after persuasion he/she should be rejected. In case
       of female candidate, medical examination will be carried out by a female medical officer. In case of non-availability of
       female medical officer, a Staff Nurse or a Female Attendant will be present when female candidates are examined by
       male medical officer.
  2.   Advice the candidate to complete the statement and declaration duly signed, in the prescribed proforma in your
       presence.
  3.   Go through the information/declaration given by the candidate and make a note of anything significant relating to
       his/her physical fitness. Countersign candidate’s declaration thereafter.
  4. Identification Mark:
               The importance of identification marks cannot be stressed too strongly. They are required for determination
               of the individuality or recognition of the person. It is a medico-legal dictum that at least two important marks
               of identification must be noted and described in detail.
               The description of the identification mark e.g. mole should include all relevant particulars such as the situation
               with reference to anatomical landmark size, color & other characteristics such as raised or flat, fixed to skin
               or free, hairy or non-hairy.
               When no distinct mark is available left hand fingers impression in males & right hand fingers impression in
               females may be taken.
  5. Expenses incurred on account of Pre Employment Medical Examination will be reimbursed by ONGC
     whether candidate found fit or unfit- Temporary/Permanent
       Note: All temporarily unfit candidates shall be given the opportunity for getting necessary medical/surgical
       management within a maximum period of 6 months and shall be re-assessed for fitness accordingly on case to case
       basis. If the candidate does not fulfil the fitness criteria even after re-assessment, he/she shall be disqualified and
       declared unfit. No further opportunity would be given.
  6. Procedures to be followed for observing various parameters and their significance has been given below:
          I)    AGE: Co-relate age as certified in the Birth date certificate with the appearance of the candidate and note
                if the candidate’s age appears to be more than what is certified.


                                                                                                                Page 20 of 32
II)    HEIGHT: The candidate’s height will be measured as follows:
       He/She will remove his/her shoes and be placed against the standard with his/her feet together and the
       weight thrown on the heels and not on the toes or on the sides of the feet. He/She will stand erect without
