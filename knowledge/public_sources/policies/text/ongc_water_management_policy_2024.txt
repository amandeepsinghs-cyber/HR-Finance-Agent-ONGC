SustainableWater Management Policy
1. OverviewandPurposeofPolicy
Oil and Natural Gas Corporate Limited(ONGC)is committed to water resource
management and efficient utilization.Sustainable water management is an integral part
of the Company's corporate vision of sustainable growth that conserves and protects
freshwaterresources andenhances theresourceefficiencyatallONGC'sassetsand
work centers. By implementing this Sustainable Water Management Policy, the
Company commits to establishing water management strategies, systems, processes,
practices, and research initiatives, all with a focus on operational eco-efficiency.
