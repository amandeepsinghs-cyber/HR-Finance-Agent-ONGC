1. PREAMBLE:
 Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC/ the Company), a Maharatna, Central Public Sector
 Enterprise, under the administrative control of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of
 India, is the leading Exploration and Production Company in India with presence in refining and marketing
 of petroleum products through subsidiaries and joint ventures.

 ONGC is a Public Limited Company and having its equity shares listed with BSE Ltd. and National Stock
 Exchange of India Limited. The Company has also issued Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and said
 NCDs are listed with BSE Ltd. Being a Listed Company, it is governed by the provisions of the Companies
 Act, 2013 (Act) and/ or guidelines/ Regulations of Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) as
 applicable for equity and Non-Convertible Securities.

 Pursuant to the requirements of Regulation 61(A)(2) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
 Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“Listing Regulations”) read with SEBI Circular dated 8 th November
 2023, the Company has framed the Policy for Dealing with Unclaimed amount towards Interest/ Dividend/
 Redemption on Listed Non-Convertible Securities (hereinafter referred to as “the Policy”).

 As per the Listing Regulations where any interest/ dividend/ redemption amount has not been claimed by
 any Non-convertible Security/ Debenture holders (“Investors”) within thirty days from the due date of
 interest/ dividend/ redemption payment, the said unpaid/ unclaimed amount shall be transferred, within
 seven days from the date of expiry of thirty days, to an escrow account to be opened by the listed entity in
 any scheduled bank in accordance with Regulation 61(A)(2) of the Listing Regulations. Further, any
 amount transferred to the escrow account that remains unclaimed/ unpaid for seven years shall be
 transferred to the ‘Investor Education and Protection Fund’ (“IEPF”) constituted in terms of Section 125 of
 the Companies Act, 2013 (the “Act”).

2. PURPOSE OF POLICY


This Policy specifies the process for dealing with unclaimed amounts i.e. Interest, Dividend, Redemption
proceeds lying with the Company in respect of non-convertible debentures/ securities for transfer of such
amounts to Escrow Account and subsequently to the Investor Education and Protection Fund including the
steps to be undertaken by the Company for processing claims of investors.

3. APPLICABILITY:

This Policy shall apply to any interest/ dividend/ redemption amount that has not been claimed by Investors
within thirty days from the due date of payment for interest/ dividend/ redemption amount.

5. OBLIGATION OF THE COMPANY:

  5.1 Transfer of unclaimed amounts to Escrow Account:

        In terms of regulation 61A (2) of the Listing Regulations, the Company shall transfer the unclaimed
        amount(s) to an Escrow Account within 7 (seven) days from the date of expiry of the 30 (thirty) days
        from the due date of interest/ dividend/ redemption.

  5.2 Interest in case of default:

        In case, a default is made in transferring the full or part amount to the Escrow Account, as referred
        to in paragraph 5.1 above, the said transfer shall be dealt as per framework for transfer of unclaimed
        amounts of applicable circular/ guidelines issued under the Listing Regulations.

  5.3 Transfer of Unclaimed Amount to IEPF:

       Amount remained unclaimed in the Escrow Account for a period of 7 (seven) years from the date it
       became due for payment/ transfer to Escrow Account, shall be transferred by the Company to the
       ‘Investor Education and Protection Fund’ as per applicable provisions and in the manner as prescribed
       under the Companies Act, 2013.

6. PROCEDURE FOR CLAIMING UNCLAIMED INTEREST /DIVIDEND/ REDEMPTION AMOUNT BY
   INVESTORS FROM ESCROW ACCOUNT


  6.1 Display of information w.r.t. unclaimed amounts on Company’s website:

        The Company, shall, within a period of 30 (thirty) days of transferring the unclaimed amount to Escrow
        Account, shall prepare and upload details of said unclaimed amount(s) in such form, manner and
        other particulars as may be prescribed under the Listing Regulations.

        Search facility for investor

        The Company shall provide details of unclaimed amount deposited in escrow account with search
        facility based on combinations of Name and PAN for convenience of investor(s) to verify if there is
        any unclaimed amount due and lying in the Escrow Account of the investor concerned.

  6.2 Procedure for filing of claim:

   a. Manner of submission of claim

      Investors (including legal heir/ successor/ nominee of such investor) shall send a duly signed request
      letter to the Nodal Officer, in the format prescribed at “Annexure I” along with all necessary enclosures
      as mentioned therein.

   b. Timeline and process for verification of claim

      Upon transfer of unclaimed amount to the Escrow account, investor(s) may file request letter along with
      prescribed documents to the Nodal Officer seeking claim of unpaid amount.

      The Company shall verify the claim upon receipt of original request letter along with prescribed
      documents. During verification, details of investors as available to the Company would be matched
      with claim documents. Reasons for change in particulars and supporting documents for change, if any,
      would be examined for approval or rejection of claim. The Company may seek additional information
      or clarification, if required. Investors shall provide the said information/ clarifications within the specified
      timeline. Failure to provide requisite information in stipulated timeline, may be liable for rejection of
      claim. However, rejection of claim does not debar an investor from filing a fresh claim.

                                                        5
 c. Timeline for processing of Claim:

     The Company shall process the claim within 30 (thirty) days of receipt of a claim documents and/or
     additional documents/ information furnished by investor and remit the payment to the investor using
     electronic modes of fund transfer.

 d. Maintenance of Records:

     The Company shall maintain documents as per requirements of applicable laws. Claim approval
     documents would be preserved for 3 (three) years from the date of settlement of claim.

 e. Display of Claims details on website:

     The Company shall display the details of the number of claims received, processed, pending, etc. on its
     website and any change in the information uploaded on the website shall be updated as per requirements.


 7. PROCEDURE FOR CLAIMING UNCLAIMED INTEREST/ REDEMPTION AMOUNT BY INVESTORS, IN
    CASE THE SAME HAS BEEN TRANSFERRED TO IEPF:


     Once unclaimed amount is transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF), procedure
     as prescribed by IEPF Authority on its website i.e. www.iepf.gov.in needs to be followed for claiming
     refund.
