A scheme for extending financial assistance to the employees retired prior to 01.01.2007 for
taking care of their emergency needs.
In terms of DPE OM No. 2(81)-08-DPE(WC)-GL-XV//2009 dated 08.07.2009 & 2(81)-08-DPE(WC)

providing benefit to retired employees for taking care of their emergency needs has been formulated.
The scheme shall be known as'Asha Kiran'
1. Objective
The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance for meeting the emergency needs of
those retired employees or their surviving spouse (in the case of deceased employees) who are not
able tocopeupwith distressful emergencysituations.
