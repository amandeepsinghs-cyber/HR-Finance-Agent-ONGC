                           EMPLOYMENT IN OIL PSUs IN ASSAM

2067. SHRI PHANI BHUSAN CHOUDHURY

                              पे टोिलयम और ाकृितक गैस मं ी

Will the Minister of PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS be pleased to state:

(a) the number of regular employees currently working under Indian Oil Corporation Limited
    (IOCL), Oil India Limited (OIL) and Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) in Assam
    separately for each Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) and by category or group, namely
    Executive, Non-Executive, Group A, Group B, Group C or equivalent;
(b) the number of employees domiciled in Assam out of the total employees separately for
    each PSU and by employee category or group;
(c) the number of employees recruited by these PSUs in Assam during the last five years,
    year-wise and category-wise;
(d) the present recruitment policy followed by IOCL, OIL and ONGC for appointments in
    Assam and whether there is any provision for giving preference to local candidates, if so,
    the details thereof; and
(e) the present vacancy position in each PSU in Assam along with the details of vacant posts,
    grade-wise and category-wise?

(d): The human resource policies of Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) are formulated
in accordance with various directives and guidelines issued from time-to-time in this regard
by the Department of Public Enterprises (DPE). The Government has delegated enhanced
powers to the Boards of Maharatna, Navratna and Miniratna CPSEs in financial and
operational matters, including HR policy. As IOCL, OIL and ONGC are Maharatna CPSEs,
their Boards are competent to formulate and implement their respective HR policies for below
Board-level employees, subject to Government guidelines.

These CPSEs comply with all statutory provisions and Government directives relating to
reservation and recruitment. Candidates belonging to Assam are encouraged to participate in
the open recruitment process and are considered for appointment subject to fulfillment of the
prescribed eligibility criteria, merit and other applicable conditions.
