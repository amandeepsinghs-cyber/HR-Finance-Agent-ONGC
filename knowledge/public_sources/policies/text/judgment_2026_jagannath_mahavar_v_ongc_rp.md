                                                          BRIEF FACTS

                         4.    The brief factual background necessary for deciding the present petition
                               is set-out below:

                               4.1.   The petitioner was working with respondent No.1 corporation
                                      since 14.07.1975 as an Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) (E-1
                                      level) and was promoted to the post of General Manager
                                      (Mechanical) (E-7 level) with effect from 01.01.1997.

                               4.2.   The next promotion for the petitioner was to the post of Group
                                      General Manager (E-8 level).

                               4.3.   The Board Sub-Committee on Departmental Promotions of
                                      respondent No.1 ('Board Sub-Committee'), also called the
                                      Departmental Promotion Committee or 'DPC', convened on
                                      11.12.2002 and 26.12.2002 to empanel candidates for the post of
                                      Executive Directors/Group General Managers. On 26.12.2002,
                                      the Executive Committee of respondent No.1 held a meeting and
                                      decided the postings of 25 candidates out a total of 31 empaneled
                                      candidates. However, the petitioner, along with 5 others, was not
                                      promoted to the post of Group General Manager.

                               4.4.    Subsequently however, on 10.03.2006, the petitioner was
                                       promoted to the post of Group General Manager (E-8 level).

                                                       DISCUSSION & CONCLUSIONS

                                      corporation since 14.07.1975 and was promoted to the level of
                                      General Manager (Mechanical) (E-7 level) with effect from
