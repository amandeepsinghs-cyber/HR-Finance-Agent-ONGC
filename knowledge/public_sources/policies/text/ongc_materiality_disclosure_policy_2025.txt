1. SCOPE / LEGAL FRAMEWORK

 This Policy has been framed as per requirements of Regulation 30(4) of the Securities and Exchange
 Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements), Regulations 2015 (hereinafter referred
 as “SEBI -LODR Regulations”) for disclosures of events/ information which, in the opinion of the Board
 of Directors of the Company, are material.

 This Policy shall be called “POLICY FOR DETERMINATION OF MATERIAL EVENTS OR
 INFORMATION FOR DISCLSOURE TO STOCK EXCHANGES” (hereinafter referred to as “the Policy”)
 of the Company for determination of materiality of events or information and disclosure thereof.

 The Policy also applies in respect of disclosure of events or information with respect to subsidiaries which
 are material for the Company.

2. OBJECTIVE

 The objective of the is Policy is to ensure continuous, adequate, accurate and timely disclosure of
 information of the Company to Stock Exchanges on an ongoing basis to enable the investors to make
 informed decisions.

 This Policy will assist the employees of the Company in identifying any potential material events or
 information that may require to be promptly reported for disclosures to Stock Exchanges to the Company
 Secretary.

 The Company shall make timely disclosures of the event/ information to the Stock Exchanges, at the time
 of occurrence of the relevant event / information and wherever applicable.

4. CLASSIFICATION OF MATERIAL EVENTS/ INFORMATION

 4.1 DEEMED MATERIAL EVENTS / INFORMATION

     Events, which are specified in Para A of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI-LODR Regulations (“Para
     A Events”) are deemed to be Material Events and shall be disclosed to Stock Exchanges without
     any application of the guidelines for materiality.

 4.2 EVENTS / INFORMATION TO BE DISCLOSED ON APPLICATION OF THE GUIDELINES FOR
     MATERIALITY

     Events, which are specified in Para B of Part A of Schedule III of the SEBI- LODR Regulations (“Para
     B Events”) shall be disclosed upon application of guidelines for materiality.

     Copy of Schedule III is enclosed (Annexure – 1) for the purpose of reference.

 5. GUIDELINES FOR DETERMINATIN OF MATERIALITY OF EVENTS / INFORMATION
 5.1 Materiality of an event/ information is to be determined on a case-to-case basis depending on specific
     facts and circumstances relating to the information/ event. Following criteria shall be applied to
     determine materiality of a particular event/ information: -
     (a) Quantitative Criteria:
         Materiality shall become applicable to an event/ information, where the value involved or
         expected impact in terms of value, exceeds the lower of the following:

                   i) 2% of turnover, as per the last audited consolidated financial statements of
                        the listed entity;
                   ii) 2% of net worth, as per the last audited consolidated financial statements of the
                        listed entity, except in case the arithmetic value of the net worth is negative;
                   iii) 5% of the average of absolute value of profit or loss after tax, as per the last
                        three audited consolidated financial statements of the listed entity.

     (b) Qualitative Criteria:-
         A. Materiality shall become applicable to an event/ information, if the omission of the event is
            likely to result in
         a. discontinuity or alteration of event or information already available publicly; or
         b. significant market reaction if the said omission came to light at a later date.

         B. In the opinion of Board of Directors of the Company, the event/ information is considered
            material.

      may be applied to determine materiality.
6. COMPETENT AUTHORITY FOR DETERMINATION OF MATERIALITY OF EVENT/ INFORMATION
   OR MATERIAL DEVELOPMENT

  The authority for determining the materiality of an event or information for the purpose of making
  disclosure to the Stock Exchanges shall be the Compliance Officer in consultation with the Functional
  Director, Director (Finance) and Chairman and CEO.

8. PROCEDURE FOR IDENTIFICATION/ REPORTING OF EVENT/ INFORMATION AND
    DISCLOSURE
   A. Identification of Event/ Information:

        a. Key Executive(s) concerned in case of the Company and Managing Director/ CEO concerned
           for respective subsidiaries shall identify and report the material event/ information to the
           Compliance Officer for disclosure to stock exchange(s);

        b. If the event/ information is UPSI, then the Executive(s) concerned to make necessary entry
           in the Structural Digital Database as maintained by the Company as per provisions of the
           SEBI (PIT) Regulations;



                                               Page 5 of 6
        If the event/ information is ascertained as the Material one, the Executive concerned shall
        prepare a draft disclosure in format prescribed wherever applicable and with consent of the
        Functional Director concerned and at-least two hours before the conclusion of the stipulated
        timelines shall share the same with the Company Secretary.

        Upon receipt of information, Compliance Officer shall consult the Director (Finance) and the
        Chairman & CEO before making disclosure of the material events/price sensitive information to
        the stock exchanges.
B. In-Charge Corporate Communications (CC):
    I. In line with the Company practice, in case the contents of event/ information to be shared for
       publication and/ or Press Release, the said communication shall be finalised by the CC with
       approval of the Chairman and to be shared with the Company Secretary before release to the
       Media.
   II. CC shall ensure release of all official Press Releases to media after submission to Stock
       Exchanges. These press releases shall also be posted on the website of the Company.
  III. CC shall ensure appropriate and fair response to queries on news reports and requests for
       verification of market rumours by regulatory authorities/ stock exchanges in consultation with
       concerned Executive(s) and Functional Director(s) to which the news report/ market rumour
       pertains.

C. Update the Status:
   In case the disclosure is made before closure/ resolution of event, necessary updates shall also be
   disclosed on a regular basis, till such time the event is resolved / closed.

9. AMENDMENT
   Amendment in Policy shall be made as under:
   a. Amendment required due to statutory modifications on account of change in law including
      clarifications issued under the SEBI - LODR Regulations or modification required for clarification
      purposes shall be appropriately factored in the Policy with the approval of the Chairman of the
      Company; and
   b. Amendment proposal not covered as per clause (i) above, shall be subject to approval of the
      Board of Directors of the Company.
