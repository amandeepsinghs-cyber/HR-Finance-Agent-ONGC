                            Utilisation of CSR Funds by ONGC

674   SHRI G M HARISH BALAYOGI:

                                     पे ो लयम एवं    ाकृ तक गैस मं ी

       Will the Minister of PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS be pleased to state:

(a) the quantum of funds utilised by ONGC under CSR during the last five years in
Andhra Pradesh, district-wise, especially in Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema district;

(b) the details and present status of projects initiated, under-completion and completed
with the CSR funds of ONGC in Andhra Pradesh, district-wise, especially in Dr. B.R.
Ambedkar Konaseema district;

(c) the details of total number of local individuals employed in CSR initiatives of ONGC
across Andhra Pradesh, especially in Dr. BR Ambedkar Konaseema district during the
last five years; and

(d) whether ONGC has any proposals for future CSR initiatives in Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Konaseema district and if so, the details thereof?

(a) to (d) Oil & Gas Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) undertake CSR activities under the
heads identified under Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 with special focus on Health
(Nutrition, Sanitation, and Drinking Water), Education, Skill Development, Rural Development,
Women Empowerment, Environment Oriented Initiatives and Care for the Elderly & Differently-
abled Persons. The Board of the Company is empowered to plan, approve, execute, and monitor
the CSR activities of the company based on the recommendations of its CSR Committee.

         ONGC has spent Rs. 88.37 crore on different projects under CSR in various districts of
Andhra Pradesh including Rs. 3.11 crore in Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema district during the
last five years (2019-20 to 2023-24). District wise-details of expenditure are as below :-
