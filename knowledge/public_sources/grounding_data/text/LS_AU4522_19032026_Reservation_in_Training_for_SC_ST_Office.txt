                             UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 4522

           RESERVATION IN TRAINING FOR SC/ST OFFICERS IN OIL PSUS


†4522. SHRI BHAUSAHEB RAJARAM WAKCHAURE:

                                  पेटोिलयम और ाकृितक गैस मं ी

          Will the Minister of PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS be pleased to state:

(a) whether there are any rules or guidelines for providing reservation to SC/ST officers in
training programmes conducted within the country and abroad in Indian Oil Corporation
Limited and other Indian Oil Companies, as per the instructions issued by the Union
Government;
(b) if so, the details thereof;

(c) the number of training programmes conducted within the country and abroad during the last
three years along with the year-wise and category-wise number of SC/ST candidates nominated
for these programmes as compared to general candidates;

(d) whether there has been any shortfall in this regard; and

(e) if so, the action proposed to be taken to provide training to candidates of the said category
within the country and abroad in accordance with the prescribed guidelines/instructions?



                                            ANSWER

                             पे ोिलयम और ाकृ ितक गैस मं ालय म रा यमं ी
                                         ( ी सुरेश गोपी)

MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM AND NATURAL GAS
                         (SHRI SURESH GOPI)



    (a)      No.
    (b)      Does not arise.
    (c)      The details of the training programmes conducted within the country and abroad
             during the last three years by the oil companies are attached as Annexure.
    (d)      No.
    (e)      Does not arise.
