STATEMENT REFERRED TO IN REPLY TO PARTS (a) TO (e) OF LOK SABHA
STARRED QUESTION NO. *401 TO BE ANSWERED ON 21.08.2025 REGARDING
“CONSULTANCY CONTRACTS BY ONGC” ASKED BY SHRI ANAND BHADAURIA


(a) to (c): No. No consultancy assignment has been awarded to M/s Beicip-Franlab in the North
Eastern Region by Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd (ONGC).
(d) to (e): Does not arise, in view of above
It is also emphasised that, ONGC is a Maharatna Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) and a
major contributor to our economy. It engages with experienced specialized entities for complex
technical studies such as modelling of permeable reservoirs, deep-water subsurface studies and
interpretation of data for production enhancement. These studies require highly specialised
domain knowledge, proprietary/patented software, advanced modelling techniques, research
infrastructure and timely delivery capabilities.
Across the world, only a handful of entities provide these specialised services. One such entity,
M/s Beicip-Franlab, operates under the control of IFPEN (Institut Français du Pétrole Energies
Nouvelles), a state of the art and world renowned Research & Development (R&D) institute
under the Ministry of Energy, Government of France. IFPEN has extensive global experience
and recognition in these specialised areas especially in deep and ultra-deep water studies,
patented proprietary software for reservoir modelling, which is technically complex. Also,
ONGC has a running Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with IFPEN (last renewed in 2023)
for joint research and technical collaboration for exploration and development of hydrocarbon
reserves and implementation of new technologies, including renewable energy.
ONGC undertakes large number of exploration and production projects across the country as per
its Board approved guidelines which are in line with the principles laid out by the General
Financial Rules (GFR) and Central Vigilance Commission (CVC). It is reiterated in reference to
(a) that no contract has been awarded to M/s Beicip-Franlab in the North Eastern Region ONGC.
Even in other regions, it has been found that the total value of the assignments awarded to M/s
Beicip-Franlab is less than Rs 6.5 crore in the last five years, which is a miniscule percentage for
this Maharatna Company that has an average Profit After Tax (PAT) of more than Rs 33,000
crore per annum (in last five years).
