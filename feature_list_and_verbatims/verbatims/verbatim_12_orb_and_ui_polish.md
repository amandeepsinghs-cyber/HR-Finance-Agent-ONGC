## 2. Core Friction & Opportunity Identified

1. **Chaotic Particle Sphere**: The current implementation renders a 640-point Fibonacci particle sphere that jitters and rotates in 3D. It feels busy, technical, and distracting rather than polished, calm, and executive.
2. **Desire for Siri / Infinity Loop Harmony**: Leadership expects a serene, organic presence—like Apple's Siri wave/infinity ribbon or DeepMind AlphaFold's smooth continuous manifolds—that pulses gently when idle and responds with harmonic amplitude to voice.
3. **Basic Frontend Visual Weight**: The application shell currently relies on standard flat Tailwind borders and cards. It needs enterprise-grade polish (refined typography, executive canvas frames, glass accents, clean white space) while strictly preserving simplicity and zero cognitive clutter.
