## 2. Core Problem & User Friction Identified

1. **Static, Unresponsive Financial Views**: The platform does not actively clarify temporal parameters (e.g. asking which fiscal year the user wants before pulling data).
2. **Missing Longitudinal Metric Analysis**: Key financial metrics like WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital) are not tracked over a 5–10 year horizon or rendered visually.
3. **Absence of Root-Cause Analysis**: Users are left to deduce why costs fluctuated across fiscal years rather than having the system isolate anomalous line items.
4. **Lack of Prescriptive Business Guidance**: Insights stop at descriptive summaries rather than providing prioritized, actionable recommendations (e.g., addressing inventory holding, renegotiating contracts).

---

## 3. Key Directives Extracted

* **Conversational Disambiguation**: When the user requests an income statement, proactively prompt for the fiscal year (current year vs. prior year) before rendering.
* **Longitudinal Trend Plotting**: Support instant computation and plotting of corporate financial health metrics (WACC, ROACE, EBITDA margins) across a 5–10 year historical window.
* **Automated Root-Cause Isolation**: Explain year-over-year cost surges by identifying specific line-item drivers (e.g. raw material inflation, depreciation spikes, exploration dry holes).
* **Prioritized Executive Action Items**: Provide a concrete list of the top 5 operational action items for leadership (CMD, Director Finance, Director HR) to mitigate cost anomalies (e.g. optimizing inventory carrying costs, standardizing procurement).
