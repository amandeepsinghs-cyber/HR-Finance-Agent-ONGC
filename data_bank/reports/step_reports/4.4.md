## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 4.4 established the semantic layer and star-schema analytical marts in BigQuery `hrfin_gold`:
- **16 Star-Schema Marts**: The 15 question-coverage marts + the cross-silo `mart_asset_productivity` (headcount × production × manpower cost by asset-year).
  - Every column required by `world_bible/question_coverage.yaml` entries exists in the corresponding Gold mart (verified across all 356 questions and follow-ups).
  - `mart_energy_transition`: Exposes all 4 technology types (`Solar PV`, `Wind Onshore`, `Wind Offshore`, `Green Hydrogen`) across all 5 fiscal years (FY22–FY26).
  - `mart_procurement_contracts`: Joined 1:1 with `po_channel` for tender channels (GeM, e-Procurement/SRM, Limited tender, Single/Proprietary, Nomination) and MSE affirmative action category.
  - `mart_workforce`: Extended with annual apprentice intake and technical training ratios.
  - `mart_hse_incident`: Extended with environmental incident metrics.
  - `mart_employee_relations`: Extended with POSH inquiry metrics (vs. 90-day statutory norm).
- **6 Semantic Views**:
  - `v_continuous_stay`: Gaps-and-islands algorithm over consecutive same-station postings.
  - `v_cluster_cumulative_tenure`: Multi-stint cumulative stay aggregation per JRTP cluster.
  - `v_headcount_asof`: As-of point-in-time active personnel aggregation for all 14 fiscal year-ends (FY13..FY26) plus current demo anchor date.
  - `v_field_pnl`: Dynamically recomputed field P&L components and loss-making / divestment candidate flags.
  - `v_lifting_cost`: Lifting cost per BOE ($/boe and ₹/boe) by field and asset across fiscal years.
  - `v_env_incidents`: Dedicated semantic view for environmental and process-safety incidents.
- **2 Expanded Metadata Tables**:
  - `glossary`: Authoritative definitions and source citations of **50 key ONGC domain terms** (exceeding target $\ge$40).
  - `metric_definitions`: **100 enterprise metrics** covering all 89 questions + 267 follow-ups + 13 addendum gap metrics (exceeding target $\ge$60).
  - **MoU Metric Disambiguation**: Defined one explicit DPE-weighted MoU composite score metric (`MET-CORP-002`) calculated as the parameter-weighted rating score sum ($\sum \text{weightage} \times \text{score} / 100$) evaluating to **4.28 for FY26** per `mou_targets`.
