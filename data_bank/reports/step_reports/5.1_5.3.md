## 3. Ground Truth Verification Highlights

1. **What-If Oil Price Shock Rebuild (W1)**:
   - Built on exact components of `hrfin_gold.v_field_pnl` / `field_pnl_annual` FY26.
   - Re-pricing formula: `oil_revenue * ((scenario_brent - avg_discount) / avg_realisation)` using `price_fx_monthly` parameters (FY26 baseline: Brent $70.90, net realization $68.40, discount $2.50).
   - Gas revenue kept flat (APM gas at $6.50/MMBtu is administered and not Brent-linked).
   - Statutory levies (Royalty & Cess) derived from implied field effective rates (`levy / gross_revenue`).
   - Operating costs and DD&A held fixed.
   - **Cross-Check Validation**: At FY26 actual prices, formula reproduces `v_field_pnl` EBITDA with **0.00% difference** across all 53 fields (`test_08_w1_formula_reproduces_v_field_pnl_ebitda` PASS).
   - **Simulation Findings at Brent $60.00/bbl**:
     - **2 fields go EBITDA-negative**: Champang (-₹15.04 Cr) and Borholla (-₹7.54 Cr).
     - **3 fields go PBT-negative**: Champang (-₹46.22 Cr), Borholla (-₹40.71 Cr), and Khoraghat (-₹33.24 Cr).
     - (Bassein remains robustly profitable with positive EBITDA due to gas revenue).
2. **Continuous Stay in Delhi & Retirement Exemption (C1, D5, P11)**:
   - Evaluated via `hrfin_gold.v_continuous_stay` and `v_cluster_cumulative_tenure`.
   - **Exemption Rule Applied**: Cites **JRTP Clause 7.0 (Pre-Retirement Tenure Exemption)** (strict 2-year statutory rule under Clause 7.1; 3-year corporate planning practice).
   - In Delhi (WC-DEL): **419 regular executives** have continuous stay $\ge$ 5 years. Accounting for **17 officers exempt under the 3-year retirement rule**, **402 executives** are due for transfer rotation.
   - In Cluster CL-DEL (Delhi NCR): **201 executives** have breached the 10-year cumulative stay cap. Subtracting **1 officer exempt under 3-year retirement practice**, **200 executives** are due for transfer.
3. **Constants Sweep**:
   - Grepped and swept all 356 queries for hardcoded magic numbers.
   - Replaced magic conversion factors with data-grounded metrics (`price_fx_monthly`, `dim_grade`, `field_pnl_annual`).
   - Removed `COALESCE(..., 50.0)` fallback and replaced with actual opex from `field_pnl_annual`.
