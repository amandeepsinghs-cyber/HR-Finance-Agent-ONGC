## 5. Potential Conflicts Between Real Excerpts and Data Patterns

An in-depth cross-examination was conducted between the authentic public excerpts and the synthetic enterprise data generated in Phase 2:
- **No fundamental legal or statutory conflicts were found.**
- **Operational Nuance in Delhi HQ Tenure (Story S1)**: The JRTP establishes a 10-year cumulative cap (Clause 4.1) and a 3–5 year normal rotation window (Clause 3.2). However, in `PA0001.csv`, 419 executives in Delhi have accumulated continuous stay exceeding 5 years, with some approaching or exceeding the 10-year cap. This apparent operational divergence is directly recognized and codified within the reconstructed rulebook under **Clause 4.3 (Corporate HQ Overstay Review)** and **Clause 4.4 (Retention Exceptions)**:
  * The policy explicitly mandates Corporate HR to compile annual overstay rosters for officers at 5, 8, and 10 years to initiate planned phased transfers;
  * It provides formal exceptions for officers within 2–3 years of retirement (Clause 7.0) and strategic national project directors approved by CMD.
  * This ensures that Question `P11`, Follow-up `T2_FU2`, and Question `H10` have solid policy footing to identify overstaying executives while explaining legitimate statutory exceptions.
