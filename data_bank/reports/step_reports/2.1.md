## 2. Defects Fixed & Architecture Overhaul

1. **Posting History Semantics (Defect 1 Fixed)**:
   - **Root Cause**: The previous generator chopped multi-decade stints into artificial 4-year chunks at the SAME work centre and typed them `rotation` or `hard_station`, producing 178,083 duplicate same-wc rows and 90,923 spurious same-wc hard station rows.
   - **Resolution**:
     - Every change of work centre now represents a **real relocation** (`prev_wc != wc_id`).
     - Consecutive same-wc rows occur **exclusively upon an in-place promotion** (`posting_type == 'promotion'`), seamlessly updating grade and designation on Infotype 0001 without resetting continuous tenure.
     - `posting_type` follows schema accepted values strictly (`initial`, `rotation`, `hard_station`, `promotion`).
     - `hard_station` is assigned **only when moving into a Category C station**.
     - Duplicate non-promotion same-wc rows = **0** (verified across all 198,880 rows).
     - In-place promotions = **62,761**.

2. **Mobility Realism & JRTP Compliance (Defect 2 Fixed)**:
   - Stint durations modeled with ~3-year minimum before routine rotation, hazard rising after 3–5 years.
   - Pre-2021 career history simulated as realistic 3.2–4.5 year stints across operational work centres respecting cluster-based cumulative caps (10 years) and hard station completion for technical cadres.
   - Non-executives remain locally bound to their home work centre throughout their careers (mean moves = 0.0 << executive mean moves = 3.85).
   - **Median stint length for executives**: **3.17 years** (avg 3.43 years), strictly within the [3.0, 5.0] year norm.
   - **Corporate HQ Tenure Stickiness**:
     - Continuous stay $\ge 5$ years (computed via gaps-and-islands over consecutive same-wc rows) at Corporate HQ (`WC-DEL`) is **363 of 695 active executives (52.2%)**, emerging squarely within the required [25%, 60%] band.

3. **Asset Headcount Dynamics (Defect 3 Fixed)**:
   - Annual locations for 2021–2026 follow field maturity and capital project activity: growth/developing assets (e.g., `WC-EAS` KG-DWN-98/2 deepwater development) expand, while mature onshore assets (`WC-MEH`, `WC-CAU`, `WC-RAJ`) decline gradually through attrition.
