# Answer Key: C13

**Question:** If we move 200 executives from Delhi/Dehradun to field assets, what changes in cost and coverage?
**Type:** `what_if` | **Result Count:** `13` rows | **Execution:** `1.67s`

## Executive Synthesis
Moving 200 E3–E5 executives from Delhi and Dehradun to field assets would cost about ₹6.40 cr one-time (₹3.20 lakh per officer in transfer grant, CTA and baggage) plus ₹2.15 cr a year in hard-station allowance, and would lift field executive coverage from 89.4% to 91.7% of sanctioned posts. Allocated pro-rata to vacancies, the biggest gains go to Neelam & Heera (+34), Bassein & Satellite (+33) and Mumbai High (+23), but 728 of the 928 field executive vacancies would remain. The pool is available: Delhi and Dehradun hold 697 E3–E5 executives, 442 of them already overdue for rotation; salaries move with the officers, so there is no payroll saving.
