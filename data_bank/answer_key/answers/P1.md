# Answer Key: P1

**Question:** What is the minimum tenure at a station, and the maximum cumulative stay in one city?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `1.35s`

## Executive Synthesis
The minimum tenure at a station is 3 completed years (JRTP 3.1), and no executive may spend more than an aggregate of 10 years in a single city or Geographic Cluster over the whole career (JRTP 4.1). Rotation is normally considered after 3 to 5 years at a work centre, and moving someone before 3 years needs administrative exigency, restructuring or disciplinary necessity approved by Director (HR). The 10-year cap is cumulative: every posting in the same station or cluster is added up irrespective of breaks or in-place promotions (JRTP 4.2).
