# Answer Key: P3

**Question:** Am I exempt from transfer if I retire in 2 years?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `1` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
Yes - an executive within 2 years of superannuation (age 58 and above) is strictly exempt from mandatory outstation transfers (JRTP 7.1), and in practice the protection extends to 3 years (age 57 and above). The exemption falls away only if you yourself ask to move to your declared home town or retirement station, or if Director (HR) or CMD orders an administrative or disciplinary transfer in writing (7.2). So an officer two years from retirement is protected unless one of those two exceptions applies.
