**Question:** Is any officer in my asset under vigilance inquiry?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `1.57s`

## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** Vigilance inquiry records, preliminary investigation reports, and complaint dossiers are strictly classified and protected against unauthorized disclosure.

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** Section 8(1)(h) of the RTI Act, 2005 (information that would impede an investigation or prosecution) and the ONGC Whistle Blower Policy / Vigil Mechanism, 2018 (P2-01) Clause 12 (Confidentiality) and Clauses 7.6 and 13.3 (identity of the Whistle Blower and the Subject kept confidential).

**Permitted Alternative:** Standard procedure for lodging vigilance complaints and Whistle Blower Policy protections.
