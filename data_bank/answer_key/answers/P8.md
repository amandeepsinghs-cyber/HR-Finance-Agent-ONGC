# Answer Key: P8

**Question:** How do I raise a concern confidentially without being victimised?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.79s`

## Executive Synthesis
Send a signed, written Protected Disclosure in a closed envelope super-scribed 'Protected Disclosure under the Whistle Blower policy' (or e-mail vigil@ongc.co.in with that subject) to the Nodal Officer - the Company Secretary, Deendayal Urja Bhawan, New Delhi - within 30 days of becoming aware (WBP 7.1-7.3). The Nodal Officer conceals your identity before forwarding and no acknowledgement is issued, to protect you; anonymous or pseudonymous complaints are not entertained (7.3-7.5). Retaliation of any kind - termination, suspension, disciplinary action, transfer, demotion or refusal of promotion - is barred, and victimisation can be reported directly to the Chairman of the Audit Committee (13.1-13.2).
