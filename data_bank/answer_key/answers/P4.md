# Answer Key: P4

**Question:** How many earned leave days can I accumulate and encash at retirement?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.64s`

## Executive Synthesis
You can accumulate up to 300 days of Earned Leave and encash up to 300 days at retirement (Leave Rules 3.3, 4.1). EL accrues at 30 days a year, credited in advance as 15 days on 1 January and 1 July, so the ceiling is reached after about 10 years without using leave; anything above the ceiling lapses. The encashment amount is (Basic Pay + IDA) / 30 x unutilised EL days, with HRA, cafeteria perks and PRP excluded (4.2).
