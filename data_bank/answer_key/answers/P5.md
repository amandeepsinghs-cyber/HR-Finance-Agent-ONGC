# Answer Key: P5

**Question:** Am I eligible for study leave, and what bond applies?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.71s`

## Executive Synthesis
You are eligible for study leave after 5 years of continuous regular service, for study directly relevant to ONGC's hydrocarbon or corporate-finance work, up to 24 months over your career (36 for an approved Ph.D.) (Leave Rules 6.1-6.3). Before leaving you must sign a service bond with two solvent sureties to serve 3 years after return; leaving within that period means refunding all leave salary, allowances, fees and travel costs plus compound interest at the bank lending rate + 2% (7.1-7.2). Study in India is sanctioned by Director (HR); study abroad needs CMD approval (6.4).
