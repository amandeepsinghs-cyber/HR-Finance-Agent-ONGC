# Answer Key: P6

**Question:** Can I accept a gift from a vendor at Diwali? What is the limit?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.62s`

## Executive Synthesis
Only within a small limit: a vendor has official dealings with you, so the relative and personal-friend exceptions do not apply, and you need the competent authority's sanction before accepting any gift worth more than rupees one thousand five hundred (DPE CDA Rule 12(4)). Several gifts from the same firm within 12 months must be reported once they add up to more than Rs.25,000/- (executives) / Rs.15000/- (non-executives), and lavish or frequent hospitality from anyone with official dealings must be avoided (Rule 12 Note). The festive exception in 12(3) covers only personal friends with no official dealings, so it does not help with vendor gifts at Diwali.
