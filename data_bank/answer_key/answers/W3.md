## Executive Synthesis
Moving 200 executives to field assets costs about ₹6.40 cr in one-time transfer grants (₹3,20,000 per officer, FY26 average) and lifts executive coverage of the 14 assets from 90.6% to 92.7%. It fills 200 of 928 executive vacancies, leaving 728; the largest gain is at Neelam & Heera (86.6% to 89.5%), while Assam and Jorhat need no one. The Delhi/Dehradun pool of 1,045 movable executives makes 200 feasible.
