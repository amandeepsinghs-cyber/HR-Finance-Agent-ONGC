## Executive Synthesis
In FY26, 9 of 15 producing assets had lifting cost above the production-weighted company average of $9.13/BOE: Jorhat Asset, Cauvery Asset, Mehsana Asset, Neelam & Heera, Rajasthan Basin, Assam Asset, Ahmedabad Asset, Mumbai High and Rajahmundry Asset. Jorhat Asset is the clear outlier at $46.30/BOE (₹4,089/BOE on only 1.78 MMboe), against $6.13/BOE at Ankleshwar Asset. Lifting cost is opex divided by BOE produced, weighted by production (field lines only); the high-volume offshore assets therefore pull the company average down.
