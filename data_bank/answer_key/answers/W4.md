## Executive Synthesis
Removing the windfall tax lifts net realisation by the full SAED per barrel: $4.00/bbl at the last levied rate (Nov-2024) and $4.91/bbl on average in the 8 months it applied in FY25. At peak it was worth $16.20/bbl (FY23, 9 months) and $10.50/bbl in FY24; SAED cost ₹10,964 cr, ₹8,634 cr and ₹3,948 cr in FY23-FY25. SAED was withdrawn in Dec-2024, so FY26 already reflects the full benefit.
