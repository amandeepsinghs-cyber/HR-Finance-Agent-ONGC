# Answer Key: P2

**Question:** Which stations count as one cluster for tenure purposes?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `13` rows | **Execution:** `0.84s`

## Executive Synthesis
ONGC has 13 Geographic Clusters; all work centres inside one cluster count as a single station for tenure, so moves within a cluster do not reset the tenure clock (JRTP 5.1-5.3). The largest is CL-MUM (Mumbai–Panvel–Uran) with 11 work centres across Mumbai, Panvel, Uran, followed by CL-CAM-ANK-VAD (8), CL-DDN (6); 5 clusters are single-site (Bokaro CBM Complex, Delhi National Capital Region, Goa Training Complex, Jodhpur Rajasthan and Tripura Agartala). Every cluster in the work-centre master is named in the JRTP 5.2 schedule, so the data and the policy agree.
