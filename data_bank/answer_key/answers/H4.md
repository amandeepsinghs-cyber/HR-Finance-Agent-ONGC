## Executive Synthesis
Yes — mid-career (E3–E5) voluntary attrition is rising sharply: 36 exits in FY24 (0.39% of opening headcount), 4 in FY25 (0.05%) and 135 in FY26 (1.67%). Total separations were 1,300, 1,600 and 1,418 in FY24–FY26, overwhelmingly superannuation; voluntary exits (resignation + VRS) were 204, 11 and 181. FY26 is the first year in the window that E4/E5 officers resigned at all (46 E4 and 30 E5 exits), signalling a new leak of experienced managers; FY25's very low voluntary count (11) looks anomalous and should be verified.
