**Question:** If we freeze hiring for 2 years, what will headcount be by discipline in 2028, given retirements?
**Type:** `what_if` | **Result Count:** `17` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
With a 2-year hiring freeze, headcount falls from 22,798 to about 21,479 by Oct-2028 (-5.8%), as 1,319 employees reach superannuation. Losses are concentrated in Production 487 (-11.5%), Drilling 377 (-11.8%), Reservoir 173 (-17.2%). Non-executive disciplines barely move because no non-executive is due to retire before 2031 in the data; other exits (resignations, deaths) are excluded, so this is an upper bound on remaining headcount.
