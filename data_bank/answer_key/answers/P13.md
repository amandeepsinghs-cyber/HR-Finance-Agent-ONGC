**Question:** Are we within the DPE 30% superannuation cap on benefits? How much headroom is left?
**Type:** `policy_plus_data` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.75s`

## Executive Synthesis
Yes: FY26 employer superannuation contributions were 27.6% of Basic + DA against the DPE 30% cap, leaving 2.4 percentage points, or ₹119 cr of headroom on a Basic + DA base of ₹4,973 cr. The mix is CPF 12.0%, PRBS 8.4%, PRMB 3.0% and gratuity 4.2%, so PRBS could be raised to its upper band without breaching the cap. FY27 to date (6 months) runs at the same 27.6%; the gratuity rate is imputed because gratuity is not booked in payroll.
