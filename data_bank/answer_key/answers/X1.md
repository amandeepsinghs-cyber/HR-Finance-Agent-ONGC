## Executive Synthesis
At 31-Mar-2026 the 15 producing assets had 20,046 contract workers against 16,327 regular employees (1.23 contractors per employee), led by Bassein & Satellite (2,690 vs 1,855). Contract labour is cheap per barrel: FY26 contractor cost was ₹546 cr versus ₹7,864 cr for regular manpower, i.e. about $0.10-2.54 per BOE for contractors against $1.32-30.90 for regular staff. The per-BOE spread is driven by volume: Ankleshwar Asset is cheapest and Jorhat Asset costliest (1.78 MMboe in FY26). Headcounts are March-2026 period-end stocks; costs are FY26 totals divided by FY26 BOE at the FY26 average exchange rate.
