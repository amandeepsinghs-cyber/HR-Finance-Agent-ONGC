# Answer Key: P10

**Question:** What are the criteria for promotion from E3 to E4?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.8s`

## Executive Synthesis
Promotion from E3 to E4 is time-bound, not vacancy-based: you need 3 to 4 completed years' residency at E3 (R&P 2.1c), a cumulative APAR rating of 'Very Good' (score ≥ 3.5 / 70%) across the residency period (3.1c), and unconditional vigilance clearance from the CVO (7.1). Everyone who meets all three is promoted, effective 1 January or 1 July after completing residency (2.2-2.3). Any sustained adverse remark or a year rated below 'Good' defers promotion by one year (3.2), and suspension, a major-penalty charge-sheet or pending prosecution bars it (7.2).
