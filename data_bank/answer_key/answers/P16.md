# Answer Key: P16

**Question:** Who has the authority to approve a ₹50 crore drilling contract under the delegated powers?
**Type:** `policy_plus_data` | **Result Count:** `4` rows | **Execution:** `0.79s`

## Executive Synthesis
A ₹50 crore drilling contract can be approved by the Functional Director concerned, with Director (Finance) concurrence (BDP Clause 3.3: drilling and well-service awards up to ₹50 crore); anything above ₹50 crore goes to the Executive Committee (CMD + all Whole-Time Directors) (BDP Clause 4.2). Because ₹50 crore is exactly the Director ceiling, any variation or price escalation would push the award into EC territory, and Clause 7.3 forbids splitting it to stay under a limit; a major deepwater rig charter is reserved for the Board regardless (Clause 6.1(b)). In practice, 10,073 drilling POs on record (₹7,687 cr) are within the Level-3 limit (largest ₹7.26 cr) and 0 have needed Director, EC or Board approval.
