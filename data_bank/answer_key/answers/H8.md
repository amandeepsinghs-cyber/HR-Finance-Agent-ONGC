**Question:** What were training hours per executive last year, and which disciplines are below target?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `16` rows | **Execution:** `0.69s`

## Executive Synthesis
In FY26 executives received 14.3 training hours each on average (1,97,900 hours across 13,878 executives on rolls); 8 of 16 disciplines are below that average, lowest Materials Management (MM) at 13.7 hours. The spread is narrow (13.7–15.0 hours, highest Human Resources (HR)); the bigger issue is coverage — only 8,745 executives (63.0%) attended any programme, and those who did averaged 22.6 hours. No discipline-level training-hour target exists in the data, so 'below target' is measured against the company executive average.
