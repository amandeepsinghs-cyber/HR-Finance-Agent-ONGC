## Executive Synthesis
FY26 CSR spend was ₹949 cr across 14 states, led by Uttar Pradesh (₹155.0 cr) and Maharashtra (₹137.6 cr); by theme, Education (₹221.9 cr) and Environmental Sustainability (₹192.8 cr) lead. Against the policy's preference for operational areas, 67.2% (₹637.3 cr) went to ONGC operational field areas and 23.4% (₹222.2 cr) to aspirational districts. The largest single state, Uttar Pradesh, has no ONGC operations: Jammu & Kashmir, Uttar Pradesh together received ₹287.6 cr, which the Board CSR Committee should be able to justify under national-priority criteria.
