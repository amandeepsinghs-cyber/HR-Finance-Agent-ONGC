## Scenario Model & Parameterized Simulation
**Model Parameters & Documented Assumptions:**

- Oil & Condensate Revenue Re-pricing: Derived using `(scenario_brent - avg_discount) / avg_realisation` from `price_fx_monthly` (FY26 baseline: Brent $70.90, net realization $68.40, discount $2.50).

- Statutory Levies: Royalty & Cess derived from implied field effective rates (`levy / gross_revenue`). SAED = $0.
- Operating Costs & DD&A: Fixed per FY26 baseline.
