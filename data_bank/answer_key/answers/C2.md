## Executive Synthesis
8,457 executives are overdue for rotation under JRTP Clause 3.2 (five or more years of continuous stay at their current work centre, excluding officers within three years of superannuation or holding a valid exemption); the largest pools sit at Neelam & Heera, Bassein & Satellite and Mehsana Asset (639, 559, 503). Only 10 hard or remote stations are short of executives against FY26 sanctioned posts, with a combined gap of 617: Neelam & Heera 158, Bassein & Satellite 155, Mumbai High 108, Eastern Offshore 82, Tripura Asset 62. The overdue pool is roughly 14 times the receiving gap, so the binding constraint is placement capacity, not a shortage of movable officers; the offshore assets themselves hold large overdue pools, so rotation has to be a swap rather than a one-way flow.
