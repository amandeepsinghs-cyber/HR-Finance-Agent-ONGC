## Executive Synthesis
Dropping from Excellent to Very Good would cut the executive PRP pool by 12.5%, about ₹39.5 cr, from ₹316.2 cr to ₹276.7 cr on FY26 payroll. The MoU rating drives half the PRP formula (eligibility falls from 100% to 75%); a further slip to Good would cost ₹79.1 cr. The MoU composite was 4.28 in FY26 and is projected at 3.88 for FY27; the estimate assumes FY26 executive PRP outgo is the Excellent-rating pool, with team and individual components unchanged.
