## Executive Synthesis
Production faces the largest retirement gap by FY30 in absolute terms (823 of 2,926 executives, 28.1%), but Drilling is hit hardest proportionally, losing 34.1% (640) of its officers by 31-Mar-2030. Across the core technical disciplines 2,254 of 7,405 executives (30.4%) retire by FY30; geoscience (Geology plus Geophysics) loses 490 and Reservoir 301. In every discipline retirements exceed the entire E0–E2 junior pipeline (e.g. Production 823 retiring vs 778 juniors), so the gap cannot be closed by promotion alone.
