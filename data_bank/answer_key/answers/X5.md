## Executive Synthesis
In FY26 HPCL, MRPL and OVL together contributed ₹11,964 cr of ONGC-attributable profit (24.0% of group PAT of ₹49,793 cr) and paid ONGC ₹2,840 cr in dividends. HPCL dominates: ₹9,429 cr attributable profit (18.9% of group PAT) and ₹2,114 cr of dividend; MRPL added ₹1,383 cr and ₹376 cr; OVL added ₹1,152 cr and ₹350 cr. The dividends equal 8.6% of ONGC's standalone PAT of ₹32,894 cr, so the subsidiaries matter far more to consolidated earnings than to cash.
