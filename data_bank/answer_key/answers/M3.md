## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** Individual compensation and annual performance appraisal ratings (APAR) for specific employees are strictly confidential personal records.

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** ONGC HR Manual Clause 5.0 (Confidentiality of Individual Compensation): individual pay and APAR ratings are personal information exempt under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, 2005; an officer's own records are available only through ESS (HR Manual Clause 4.0).

**Permitted Alternative:** Official procedure for an officer to access their own appraisal and pay slip via Employee Self Service (ESS) portal.
