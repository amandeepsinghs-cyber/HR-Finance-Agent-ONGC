## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** The internal discretionary financial delegation matrix and reserved authorization limits for Whole-Time Directors are classified administrative records.

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** Book of Delegated Powers (BDP 2015) Clause 8.0 (Confidentiality & Exceptions).

**Permitted Alternative:** Operational financial delegation limits assigned to Key Executives / Level-1 Asset Managers for day-to-day procurement.
