# Answer Key: P12__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How does the promotion clearance rate compare between officers who fulfilled the hardship criteria versus those with waivers?
**Type:** `policy_plus_data` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.64s`

## Executive Synthesis
Officers who met the 3-year hard/NE norm cleared E4→E5 at 93.3%, lower than those who did not (98.2% with under 3 years, 99.5% with no hard posting). So the prerequisite is not being enforced: 380 officers were promoted since FY22 without completing it, and no hardship waiver is recorded for any of them. Of 2,227 cases, the 128 pending ones are stuck on court stays and vigilance clearance rather than on hardship tenure.
