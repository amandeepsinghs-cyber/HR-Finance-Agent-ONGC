**Question:** How does the statutory government take percentage on ONGC nomination fields compare with NELP/HELP revenue-sharing contracts?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.67s`

## Executive Synthesis
Statutory government take on ONGC nomination fields was 20.5% of gross revenue in FY26 (royalty 10.4% + cess 10.1%), and 20.8% over FY22-FY26 including windfall tax. The only non-nomination fields in the data, the 2 Panna-Mukta joint-venture fields, paid 24.9% (24.6% over five years), mostly from a higher cess share (14.3% vs 10.1%). NELP/HELP blocks, profit petroleum and revenue-share payments are not in the data, so a true NELP/HELP comparison cannot be made; these figures cover royalty, cess and SAED only.
