# Answer Key: P2__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** What is the policy consequence on station tenure calculation if an officer moves within the same cluster?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
Moving within the same cluster has no tenure effect - the clock keeps running, because it is not an outstation transfer and neither station tenure nor cumulative cluster tenure resets (JRTP 5.3). The officer stays on HR's annual cumulative-stay roster (5, 8, and 10-year milestones) and at 10 years in the cluster goes onto the mandatory outstation transfer list (4.2-4.3). In practice a Delhi-to-Noida or WOB-to-Uran move cannot be used to satisfy the 3-year rotation norm (3.1-3.2).
