# Answer Key: C5__why

**Question:** Why has low executive trainee recruitment between FY20 and FY25 exacerbated the mid-career seniority vacuum?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `12` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
Core technical executive intake collapsed in FY20–FY25: 132 officers joined Drilling, Geoscience, Reservoir and Production over six years (about 22 a year) versus 1,687 in FY14–FY19 (about 281 a year). None of the FY20–FY25 joiners is still in service, so the cohort that should be reaching E3–E4 by FY30 is effectively missing, exactly when the C5 retirements peak. With no mid-career bench, each senior retirement must be filled by accelerated promotion of a thin E3–E5 layer or lateral hiring, which is the seniority vacuum the question describes.
