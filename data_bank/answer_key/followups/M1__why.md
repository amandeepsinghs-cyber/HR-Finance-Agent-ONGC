# Answer Key: M1__why

**Question:** Why is aggregate asset-level headcount accessible to HR officers but restricted from general Finance officers?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.62s`

## Executive Synthesis
No document in the policy corpus explicitly grants aggregate asset headcount to HR officers while denying it to Finance officers; the restriction rests on role-based, need-to-know controls in JRTP Clause 11.0 (Confidentiality of Pre-Decisional Records) and HR Manual Clause 5.0 (Confidentiality of Individual Compensation). The closest rule is the JRTP's requirement that HR record systems be restricted to authorised Corporate HR personnel ("11.1 Absolute Confidentiality of Draft Proposals: All preliminary transfer sheets, draft rosters, internal deliberations of the Transfer Screening Committee,..."). Treat the HR-vs-Finance split for aggregate headcount as an access-design choice, not a cited rule (documentation gap).
