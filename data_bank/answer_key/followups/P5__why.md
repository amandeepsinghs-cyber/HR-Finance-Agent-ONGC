# Answer Key: P5__why

**Question:** Why does ONGC require execution of a mandatory service bond with financial surety prior to study leave approval?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
The bond protects ONGC's investment: an officer on study leave keeps drawing leave salary and allowances while ONGC pays tuition, books and travel, so the rules bind the officer to serve 3 years after return (Leave Rules 7.1) and claw everything back with compound interest at the bank lending rate + 2% on breach (7.2). Because leave can never be claimed as of right (1.3), ONGC can make study leave conditional on that bond and two solvent sureties.
