**Question:** How does the asset-wise headcount growth compare with the 5-year oil and gas production growth rate across those same assets?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `16` rows | **Execution:** `0.79s`

## Executive Synthesis
Headcount has fallen faster than production almost everywhere: across the producing assets headcount changed -21.8% (Mar-21 to Mar-26) against -6.3% for oil and condensate, -12.5% for gas and -9.5% in BOE (FY21 to FY26). 12 of the 14 assets with FY21 output cut headcount by more than their BOE decline (widest gaps: Neelam & Heera -29.1% heads vs -10.6% BOE, Ahmedabad Asset -27.9% heads vs -13.6% BOE), so output per head has improved. Eastern Offshore had no FY21 production (output 28.1 MMboe in FY26) and is the only asset where both headcount and output grew.
