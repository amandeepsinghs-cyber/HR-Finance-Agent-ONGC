**Question:** What logistical or facility barriers (accommodation, medical support) impact female officer deployment in offshore/remote fields?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.67s`

## Executive Synthesis
The document corpus contains no policy on offshore accommodation or deployment facilities for women officers, so the barriers cannot be evidenced from policy (data gap). The only facility-related provision found is in ONGC Medical Examination of Employees Rules, 2026 (Part 4): "NOTE: For women candidates, a female medical officer will conduct the examination. If a female medical officer is not available at the work center, the examination may be conducted by a male medical officer in the presence of a female nurse or attendant." (P1-03 Part 28 repeats the requirement.) Combined with women's very low offshore share (see H7 drill-down), female medical cover at remote sites is the one documented dependency to check first.
