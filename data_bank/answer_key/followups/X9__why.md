**Question:** Why did certain non-operational states receive CSR project funding under national priority and disaster relief allocations?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `8` rows | **Execution:** `0.64s`

## Executive Synthesis
8 FY26 projects worth ₹311.7 cr sit outside operational field areas, 7 of them in states with no ONGC operations (Jammu & Kashmir, Uttar Pradesh). The stated justification in the data is national priority: 6 of the 8 are in aspirational districts (₹222.2 cr), consistent with CSR Policy 2025 allowing projects anywhere in India 'with preference to its Operational Areas'. The rest - e.g. Specialized Equipment for District Civil Hospitals in Anantnag - have no aspirational or disaster-relief tag and were sponsored from WC-DDN, WC-DEL, so their rationale is not evidenced in the data.
