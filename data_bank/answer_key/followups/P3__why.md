# Answer Key: P3__why

**Question:** What operational guidelines govern the retention or preferred posting of executives approaching retirement?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.64s`

## Executive Synthesis
Executives near retirement are ordinarily kept at their current station to allow a smooth superannuation transition, pension settlement, family stabilisation and medical management (JRTP 7.1). They may still request a transfer to their declared home town or chosen retirement station (7.2a), and only a written administrative or disciplinary order from Director (HR) or CMD can override the protection (7.2b). Compliance is reported as high: non-initial transfers within 3 years of retirement are under 2.0% of transfer actions in SAP records (7.3).
