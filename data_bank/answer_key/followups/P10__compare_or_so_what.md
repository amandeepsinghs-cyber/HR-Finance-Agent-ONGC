# Answer Key: P10__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How do promotion criteria compare between engineering disciplines and support services (HR, MM, Finance)?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.66s`

## Executive Synthesis
Up to E4 the criteria are identical for engineering and support disciplines - the same residency, APAR benchmark and vigilance clearance, with no vacancy constraint (R&P 2.2). The divergence starts at E4 to E5: technical cadres (Drilling, Production, Reservoir, Geology, Geophysics, Engineering) must first complete a 3-year Category 'C' hard-station tenure or be bypassed by the DPC (5.1-5.2), whereas HR, MM and Finance are not in that list. The merit matrix (50 APAR / 30 interview / 10 qualifications / 10 seniority) and the 1:3 zone of consideration apply to all disciplines, with seniority reckoned within each discipline (4.2-4.3).
