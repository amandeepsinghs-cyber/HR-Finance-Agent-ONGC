# Answer Key: P4__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How does the encashment formula at retirement compare with active service leave encashment during LFA/LTC?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.64s`

## Executive Synthesis
At retirement you encash up to 300 days in one lump sum at (Basic Pay + IDA) / 30 per day (Leave Rules 4.2); during service you may encash at most 30 days once a calendar year and must keep at least 30 days in balance (4.3). The rate is the same pay-based formula, so the difference is volume and timing, not value per day. The corpus has no LFA/LTC-linked encashment rule - running encashment under 4.3 is the only in-service provision.
