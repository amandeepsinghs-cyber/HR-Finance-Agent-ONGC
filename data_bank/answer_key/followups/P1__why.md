# Answer Key: P1__why

**Question:** Why does the JRTP establish a 10-year cumulative city tenure cap for executives?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.69s`

## Executive Synthesis
JRTP gives no stand-alone reason for the 10-year cumulative cap; its rationale is the policy's own objectives in Clause 1.3 - broadening multi-basin exposure for leadership development and ensuring equitable sharing of arduous and remote postings - which an indefinitely long stay in one city would defeat. Clause 4.3 operationalises it: Corporate HR lists officers at 5, 8, and 10 years of cumulative stay every year and puts those at 10 years or more (notably Delhi-NCR and Mumbai Urban) on the mandatory outstation transfer list. Retention beyond the cap is allowed only for superannuation within 2 to 3 years, certified severe medical conditions, or CMD approval for vital national hydrocarbon projects (4.4).
