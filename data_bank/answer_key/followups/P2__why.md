# Answer Key: P2__why

**Question:** Why are contiguous work centres grouped into unified clusters to prevent superficial rotational transfers?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.66s`

## Executive Synthesis
Contiguous work centres are grouped so that a move within one urban or industrial agglomeration cannot pass as rotation: an intra-cluster transfer (from New Delhi to Noida, or from Ahmedabad Asset to IRS Chandkheda, or from Mumbai WOB to Uran Plant) does NOT reset station tenure or cumulative cluster tenure (JRTP 5.3). Clause 4.2 then adds up every historical posting in the same station or cluster irrespective of breaks or in-place promotions, so the cumulative cap cannot be dodged by short hops. A cluster is defined as contiguous stations within a defined industrial or urban agglomeration (5.1).
