**Question:** Why does the projected retirement cliff disproportionately deplete executive technical talent in senior grades?
**Type:** `what_if` | **Result Count:** `9` rows | **Execution:** `0.66s`

## Executive Synthesis
The data does not show a senior-grade cliff: only 2.9% of technical executives in E7-E9 retire by Oct-2028, versus 12.5% in E1-E6, and the highest rate is in E6 (Joint General Manager (JGM) / Addl. GM, 14.0%). In absolute terms the loss is 1,285 officers in E1-E6 against 27 in E7-E9, so a freeze erodes the middle-management technical bench (E5-E6 feeders) more than the GM/CGM layer. Caveat: grade and age are weakly linked in the data (average age of E1 officers is 43.4 years), so the premise may hold in reality but is not supported by these records.
