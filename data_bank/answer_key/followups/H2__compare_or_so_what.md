**Question:** How does the percentage of hard-station unserved officers in HR and Finance compare with Drilling and Production disciplines?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `5` rows | **Execution:** `0.68s`

## Executive Synthesis
HR and Finance executives are far more likely never to have served a hard station — 13.9% (HR) and 12.4% (F&A) versus 1.7% (Drilling) and 1.9% (Production), about 7.2 times the rate. In absolute terms HR and F&A account for 173 never-hard executives versus 87 in Drilling and Production combined, despite having far fewer officers. The company average is 6.3% of 13,453 executives — a fair-rotation drive should start with corporate support cadres.
