**Question:** Who is the designated Chief Vigilance Officer (CVO) / Nodal Officer for receiving Whistle Blower disclosures?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.79s`

## Executive Synthesis
Whistle Blower disclosures are received by the Nodal Officer - the Company Secretary (WBP 4.8) - at vigil@ongc.co.in / Deendayal Urja Bhawan, New Delhi (7.1), not by the CVO. They are dealt with by the Competent Authority, i.e. the Director (HR) or a Functional Director nominated by the CMD (4.4); the Chief Vigilance Officer appears only as one of the possible Investigators (4.7). The corpus does not name the current CVO or Company Secretary.
