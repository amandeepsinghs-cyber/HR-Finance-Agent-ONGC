# Answer Key: F7__why

**Question:** Why has employee cost per unit revenue risen faster in legacy onshore assets compared to high-volume offshore platforms?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `4` rows | **Execution:** `0.63s`

## Executive Synthesis
Per barrel, onshore manpower cost rose from ₹379 to ₹393/BOE between FY22 and FY26 while offshore fell from ₹210 to ₹198, because onshore production dropped 123.0→102.2 MMboe while offshore held at ~194.4 MMboe. Offshore platforms produce 32,663 BOE per employee against 9,850 onshore, so the same pay increases weigh about 3.3x as heavily on onshore barrels. Measured against revenue the ratio actually fell in both segments (onshore 11.5%→9.2%, offshore 6.5%→4.7%) as prices rose, so the 'faster rise' holds per BOE, not per rupee of revenue.
