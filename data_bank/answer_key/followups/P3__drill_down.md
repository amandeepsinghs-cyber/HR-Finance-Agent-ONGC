**Question:** What is the exact superannuation exemption cut-off window (e.g. 2 years vs 3 years) under the JRTP?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
The binding cut-off is 2 years (age 58 and above): executives that close to superannuation are strictly exempt (JRTP 7.1); the 3-year window (age 57 and above) is organisational practice, under which officers are only 'ordinarily' not moved. The wider window recurs elsewhere in the policy - superannuation within 2 to 3 years is a ground for retention beyond the cumulative cap (4.4a) and superannuation within 2–3 years a ground for representation to the TGRC (13.2d).
