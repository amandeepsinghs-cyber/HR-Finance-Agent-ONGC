## Executive Synthesis
A freeze to end-FY28 barely changes the reservation mix: the largest shift is General at 41.55% to 41.47% of the workforce, because retirements (4.0%-4.6% by category) fall evenly. The real hit is the leadership bench: E6 loses 8.6% (98 of 1,146), and E5-E9 together lose 281. Rosters stay compliant in share terms, but backlog reserved vacancies cannot be filled while hiring is frozen.
