# Answer Key: C12__why

**Question:** Why is manpower cost per BOE significantly higher at mature, low-volume onshore fields compared to offshore assets?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `5` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
It is a volume effect, not a pay effect: mature onshore fields cost $4.65 of manpower per BOE vs $2.19 for mature offshore fields, even though each onshore employee costs less (₹38.3 lakh vs ₹65.0 lakh a year). The difference is output per head: 9,330 BOE per employee onshore vs 33,553 offshore, because onshore output is spread over 35 small fields averaging 2.45 MMboe each against 15.97 MMboe per offshore field. Each onshore field needs its own minimum crew for wells, gathering stations and water handling regardless of volume, so cost per barrel rises as fields decline.
