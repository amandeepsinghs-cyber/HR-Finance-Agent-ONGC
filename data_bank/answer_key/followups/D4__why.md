**Question:** Why is field-level P&L and forward capex planning restricted to senior management?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `1` rows | **Execution:** `0.63s`

## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** Field-level profitability statements and draft forward capital budget allocations are commercially sensitive operational data restricted to senior management.

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** Book of Delegated Powers (BDP) Clause 8.0 (Commercial Confidentiality of Operational Schedules, 8.1–8.2) and Clause 6.1(c)–(d) (annual capex budget and field divestment/monetisation reserved for the Board); field-level commercial data is protected under Section 8(1)(d) of the RTI Act, 2005 (commercial confidence).

**Permitted Alternative:** Audited company-wide gross revenues, standalone capital expenditure, and net profit from published annual accounts.
