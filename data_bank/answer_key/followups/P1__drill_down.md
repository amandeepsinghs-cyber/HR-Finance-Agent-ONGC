**Question:** What are the specific tenure limits for hard stations (Assam, Tripura, Silchar) versus normal stations?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
JRTP does not set a different maximum for hard stations: the same 3-year minimum and 3 to 5-year rotation window apply everywhere, but every technical or operational executive must complete at least one full 3-year continuous tenure in a Category 'C' hard station (Assam Asset, Tripura Asset, Bokaro CBM, Rajasthan Jodhpur, or 14/14 Offshore Duty) before E4-to-E5 merit promotion (JRTP 6.1). Normal-station tenure can be stretched to 5 years for critical technical roles (3.3), while hard-station service earns a Special Hard Station Allowance of 10% of basic pay, retention of family accommodation at the last station and extra leave/travel concessions (6.3). Category C (JRTP 2.1c) covers the Assam asset, the Tripura asset (Agartala, Silchar), Bokaro, Jodhpur and offshore installations; note that the cluster schedule (5.2) groups Silchar with Assam rather than Tripura.
