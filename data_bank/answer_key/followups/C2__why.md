**Question:** Why have previous annual transfer cycles left North-East work centres persistently below sanctioned executive strength?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `5` rows | **Execution:** `0.76s`

## Executive Synthesis
Every recent transfer cycle has moved more executives out of the North-East than into it: across FY22–FY26, 458 executives were posted into NE work centres and 831 out, a cumulative net loss of 373. Outflows (officers completing NE tenures) consistently outrun replacement postings, so the gap is a structural replacement shortfall in each Annual General Transfer rather than a one-off event. Against FY26 sanctions the NE executive gap is 62 of 1,921 posts; only FY26 sanctioned strength is recorded, so earlier-year shortfalls cannot be measured directly.
