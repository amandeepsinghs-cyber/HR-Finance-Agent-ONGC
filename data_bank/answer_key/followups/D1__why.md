**Question:** Why is individual officer salary confidential under ONGC policy and CDA rules?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
Individual officer salary is confidential because the HR Manual treats colleagues' and supervisors' pay, allowances and ratings as protected personal information and prohibits employees and automated tools from querying or disclosing them without statutory authorisation. So the assistant must refuse the reporting-officer salary request and offer only published grade scales. Cited: HR Manual Clause 5.0 (Confidentiality of Individual Compensation); HR Manual Clause 2.0 (IDA Pay Scales by Grade).
