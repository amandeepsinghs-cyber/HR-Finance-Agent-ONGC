# Answer Key: P3__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How does the transfer exemption policy apply to medical hardship or spouse-ground cases compared to retirement?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
Retirement protection is automatic, while medical and spouse grounds must be applied for and adjudicated: within 2 years of superannuation an executive is strictly exempt (JRTP 7.1), but a medical case needs Standing Medical Board certification and Special Review Board approval, valid for only 1 to 2 years with annual re-evaluation (8.1-8.3). A working spouse in government or a CPSE at the station is not an exemption at all - only a ground for representation to the TGRC within 15 days of the order, disposed of within 30 days, with no automatic stay (13.1-13.3).
