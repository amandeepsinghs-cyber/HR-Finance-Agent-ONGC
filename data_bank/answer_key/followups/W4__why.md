# Answer Key: W4__why

**Question:** Why did the Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED) previously compress ONGC net realizations below international benchmarks?
**Type:** `what_if` | **Result Count:** `5` rows | **Execution:** `0.71s`

## Executive Synthesis
SAED was a specific per-barrel levy that rose with international prices, so it pulled realisation away from Brent exactly when prices peaked: in FY23 ONGC realised $75.80 against Brent $95.40, a $19.60 gap versus $2.28 the year before. It took ₹10,964 cr (7.05% of revenue) in FY23 and ₹8,634 cr in FY24. From FY24 the realisation series no longer nets SAED out (gap $2.43), a data inconsistency to keep in mind.
