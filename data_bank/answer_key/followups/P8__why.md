# Answer Key: P8__why

**Question:** Why does the Whistle Blower policy provide explicit safeguards against reprisal and retaliatory service transfers?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
The safeguards exist so that employees report genuine misconduct 'without fear of punishment/ victimization or unfair treatment' - a vigil mechanism only works if reporting carries no career risk (WBP 3.2-3.3). That is why 13.1 expressly protects against retaliatory transfer, demotion, refusal of promotion, suspension or termination. The protection is not a shield for abuse: mala fide, frivolous or knowingly false disclosures attract disciplinary action and disqualify the complainant from further disclosures (14.1-14.3).
