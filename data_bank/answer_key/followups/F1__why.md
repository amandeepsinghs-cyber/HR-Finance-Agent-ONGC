**Question:** Why has the lifting cost in mature fields like Cambay and Mehsana increased over the last 3 fiscal years?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `6` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
Lifting cost in rupee terms rose between FY24 and FY26: Cambay Asset ₹760→₹795/BOE (+4.6%); Mehsana Asset ₹1,229→₹1,327/BOE (+8.0%). The cause is a largely fixed cost base spread over declining output - Cambay Asset production 6.73→5.79 MMboe (-14.0%) while opex moved only ₹511 cr→₹460 cr and water cut rose 42.9%→45.5%; Mehsana Asset production 10.34→9.24 MMboe (-10.6%) while opex moved only ₹1,272 cr→₹1,227 cr and water cut rose 85.5%→86.8%. In dollars the rise is masked by rupee depreciation (Cambay Asset $9.18→$8.99; Mehsana Asset $14.85→$15.03 per BOE), so the trend should be tracked in ₹/BOE.
