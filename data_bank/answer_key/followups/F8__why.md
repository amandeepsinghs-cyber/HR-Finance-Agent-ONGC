# Answer Key: F8__why

**Question:** Why have complex HP-HT and exploratory drilling dry holes elevated exploration costs in frontier basins?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `6` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
Exploration cost is highest where wells are deepest and dry-hole rates highest: in Krishna Godavari (deepwater) an exploratory well averaged ₹298.7 cr at 5,055 m, and 61.5% of 13 wells in FY24-FY26 were dry, writing off ₹2,264 cr. Across all basins dry holes cost ₹10,988 cr, the largest share in Western Offshore (₹6,142 cr). There is no HP-HT flag in the well data, so the HP-HT effect cannot be isolated; depth and terrain are the available proxies.
