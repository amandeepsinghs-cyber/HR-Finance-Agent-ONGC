# Answer Key: C11__why

**Question:** Why did surface engineering and administrative staffing expand despite continuous reservoir pressure and production declines?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `4` rows | **Execution:** `0.78s`

## Executive Synthesis
They did not expand: at the 14 assets whose production fell, surface engineering staff went from 6,716 to 5,860 (-12.7%) and administrative & support staff from 1,697 to 531 (-68.7%) between March 2021 and March 2026. What did rise is surface engineering's share of the workforce (33.7% → 38.3%), because subsurface, drilling and production staff were cut harder (-23.2%). The question's premise comes from the earlier answer key, which summed twelve monthly headcount snapshots; on period-end headcount no asset or support function expanded.
