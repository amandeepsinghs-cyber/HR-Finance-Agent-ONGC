## Executive Synthesis
Delhi HQ's five-year retention rate is lower than the other corporate offices: 60.5% of its 692 executives have stayed five years or more, versus 76.5% at Dehradun Tel Bhavan and 77.4% at Regional Office Mumbai. The company-wide rate is 74.7% (average continuous stay 5.3 years vs 4.8 in Delhi), so Delhi's issue is the size of its long-stay pool (419 executives) rather than an unusually high rate. Tel Bhavan has an even larger long-stay block (455 executives) and should be included in any HQ rotation drive.
