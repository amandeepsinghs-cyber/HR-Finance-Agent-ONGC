**Question:** What is the authorized procedure for an executive to view their own APAR grading and salary slip on the ESS portal?

## Executive Synthesis
An executive views their own APAR grading and salary slip only through the authenticated Employee Self-Service (ESS) portal, per HR Manual Clause 4.0 (Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal Access). ESS access is limited to the employee's own payslip/Form 16, leave, e-APAR scorecards, PF/pension balances and posting history: "4.1 Authorized Digital Channel: The Employee Self-Service (ESS) web portal is the sole authorized digital system for employees to access their individual employment and payroll records. 4.2 Scope of..." No other channel (including this assistant) may return another employee's records.
