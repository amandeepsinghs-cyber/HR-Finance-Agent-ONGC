**Question:** Why does the statutory POSH policy mandate time-bound inquiry completion within 90 days?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `6` rows | **Execution:** `0.75s`

## Executive Synthesis
The 90-day limit exists to force timely closure, and ONGC's own record shows why it matters: the share of POSH inquiries closed within 90 days fell from 100% in FY22 to 58% in FY26, when the longest inquiry took 130 days (worst year: FY26). Over FY22-FY27, 47 of 55 closed cases met the 90-day norm (85.5%). The statutory text behind the rule is not in the document corpus, so the 'why' is evidenced from case data rather than cited.
