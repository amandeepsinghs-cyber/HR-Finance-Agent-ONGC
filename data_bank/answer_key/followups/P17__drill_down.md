## Executive Synthesis
For FY26 the DIPAM minimum on the 30%-of-PAT test is ₹9,868 cr; ONGC paid ₹16,669 cr, ₹6,801 cr above it (50.7% of PAT). The margin over the floor rose from ₹1,117 cr in FY22, so the floor is not the binding constraint. The 5%-of-net-worth test cannot be computed because net worth is not in the data, and DIPAM requires the higher of the two.
