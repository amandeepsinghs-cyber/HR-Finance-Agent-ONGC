# Answer Key: D3__why

**Question:** Why are DPC committee deliberations and promotion candidate lists classified as confidential?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.63s`

## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** Departmental Promotion Committee (DPC) deliberations, assessment rosters, and promotion panel recommendations are classified confidential records.

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** ONGC R&P/Promotion rules Clause 8.0 (Confidentiality of DPC Proceedings & Sealed Cover, 8.1–8.2): DPC minutes, candidate scoring sheets and recommendations are privileged until the panel is released; individual assessments are also personal information under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, 2005.

**Permitted Alternative:** Published promotion policy residency requirements and benchmark APAR eligibility criteria by grade.
