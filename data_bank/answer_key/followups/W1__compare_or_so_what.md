## Executive Synthesis
At $60 Brent, ONGC's annual EBITDA would fall by about ₹11,121 cr, from ₹63,284 cr to ₹52,163 cr (-17.6%). Revenue drops ₹14,468 cr; with opex fixed, pre-tax operating cash flow falls by the same ₹11,121 cr. Gas is APM-priced and unchanged; post-tax cash flow and working-capital effects are not modelled.
