# Answer Key: P18__why

**Question:** Why is the draft transfer proposal strictly confidential prior to official approval by Director (HR) and CMD?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `1` rows | **Execution:** `0.62s`

## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** Annual rotational transfer orders in draft proposal stage are privileged administrative deliberations pending formal approval by Director (HR) and CMD.

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** Job Rotation and Transfer Policy (JRTP) Clause 11.0 (Confidentiality & Promulgation of Transfer Orders).

**Permitted Alternative:** Published JRTP rotation calendar, station tenure norms (Clause 3.0), and hard station service rules.
