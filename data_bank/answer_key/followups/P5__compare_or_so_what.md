# Answer Key: P5__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How does the study leave duration and allowance compare between domestic universities and premier overseas institutions?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `1` rows | **Execution:** `0.68s`

## Executive Synthesis
The Leave Rules make no domestic/overseas distinction on duration: study leave is capped at 24 months over a career (36 for a Ph.D.) wherever the course is (Leave Rules 6.3); the only difference is the sanctioning authority - Director (HR) for study in India, CMD for study abroad (6.4). No higher allowance for premier overseas institutions is specified anywhere in the corpus, so that premise cannot be supported from ONGC rules.
