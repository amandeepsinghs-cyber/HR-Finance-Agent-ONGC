## Executive Synthesis
The text of the Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act is not in the document corpus, so it cannot be quoted; the closest ONGC authorities are J-14 (ONGC Employees Mazdoor Sabha v. ONGC - Section 4 of that Act casts a duty on the employer in every), J-17 (Engineering Mazdoor Sangh v. ONGC (Part 5)), P2-17 (Supplier Code of Conduct (Part 2)). The Gujarat High Court record (J-14) shows ONGC deploying contract labour in its Western Region for long periods after recruitment stopped, and the tribunal matter (J-17) turned on ONGC giving work to contractors in preference to casual workmen - both show that core, perennial work done through contractors is open to regularisation claims. The Supplier Code of Conduct (P2-17) binds contractors to applicable labour laws. Statutory CLRA provisions should be confirmed with Legal before relying on this answer.
