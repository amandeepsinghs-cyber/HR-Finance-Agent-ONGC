**Question:** What is the maximum limit on earned leave accumulation (e.g. 300 days) and half pay leave encashment at superannuation?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.8s`

## Executive Synthesis
The EL accumulation ceiling is 300 days (Leave Rules 3.3); credits above it are parked in a reserve and lapse on 30 June / 31 December if unused (3.4). Half Pay Leave accrues at 20 days per completed year (5.1) and can be encashed at superannuation only to bridge an EL shortfall up to the combined 300-day aggregate, at two days of HPL per day of cash salary (5.3). So total leave encashment at retirement never exceeds 300 days, whatever the EL/HPL mix.
