## Executive Synthesis
The SAED removal in December 2024 eliminated a $4.91/bbl levy (Apr-Nov 2024 average) on domestic crude, which is the incremental per-barrel benefit. The recorded net realisation does not show a step-up: it was 97.0% of Brent before and 97.4% after (discount $2.42 vs $2.06), because the FY25 realisation series is not stated net of SAED. The benefit therefore shows up in lower statutory levies, not in the published realisation figure.
