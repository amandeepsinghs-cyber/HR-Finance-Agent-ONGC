# Answer Key: E5__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How does the offshore boarding and lodging allowance compare with onshore Class-A city travel entitlements?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `10` rows | **Execution:** `0.81s`

## Executive Synthesis
Onshore, an E2 officer touring a Class-A city may claim hotel lodging up to ₹4,500 a day and travels Economy_Air. Offshore there is no lodging claim: where board and lodging are provided free, as on an installation, Daily Allowance is paid at only 0.25 of the normal rate (TA Regulations, free board-and-lodging rule). In practice offshore duty costs the officer nothing for stay but cuts DA, while onshore tours reimburse actual lodging up to the cap. Class-A lodging caps across executive grades range from ₹4,500 to ₹10,000.
