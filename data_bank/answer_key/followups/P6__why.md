# Answer Key: P6__why

**Question:** Why do CVC regulations require prompt reporting and surrender of commercial vendor gifts exceeding nominal value?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.75s`

## Executive Synthesis
The conduct rules treat vendor gifts as a corruption risk: taking any bribe or illegal gratification is misconduct, and soliciting donations or sponsorship from contractors or vendors for an employee-linked NGO is misconduct too (DPE CDA Rule 5). Every employee must not place themselves under any obligation that could influence official duties, nor misuse their position for personal benefit (Rule 4(1)(xiv)-(xv)), and must avoid lavish or frequent hospitality from firms with official dealings (Rule 12 Note). The specific CVC circular on reporting and surrendering gifts is not in the corpus; prompt reporting is simply how these rules keep any acceptance transparent.
