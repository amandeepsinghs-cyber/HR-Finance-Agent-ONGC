# Answer Key: P4__why

**Question:** Why are leave encashment calculations governed by DPE and income tax statutory caps?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.65s`

## Executive Synthesis
The Leave Rules do not state the rationale; they fix a 300-day encashment ceiling and a (Basic Pay + IDA) formula (4.1-4.2) and make HPL encashment explicitly subject to DPE guidelines (5.3) - i.e. ONGC, as a CPSE, keeps terminal leave benefits inside the DPE pay-and-perks framework. The income-tax exemption limit on leave encashment is not in the document corpus, so the tax side of the question cannot be answered from ONGC sources. Excluding HRA, perks and PRP from the base keeps the liability tied to basic pay plus IDA only.
