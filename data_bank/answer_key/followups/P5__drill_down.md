# Answer Key: P5__drill_down

**Question:** What are the qualifying service criteria and approved postgraduate degree specializations for executive study leave?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `1` rows | **Execution:** `0.63s`

## Executive Synthesis
The qualifying service is a minimum of 5 years of continuous regular service in ONGC (Leave Rules 6.2). The rules do not list approved degree specialisations; they only require postgraduate study, an executive fellowship or research directly relevant to hydrocarbon exploration, reservoir development, drilling engineering or corporate finance (6.1). A named-specialisation list would have to come from an HR circular that is not in the corpus.
