# Answer Key: P10__drill_down

**Question:** What is the minimum qualifying residency period and aggregate APAR score threshold for E3 to E4 advancement?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.7s`

## Executive Synthesis
Minimum qualifying residency for E3 to E4 is 3 to 4 completed years at E3 (R&P 2.1c), and the APAR threshold is a cumulative 'Very Good' (score ≥ 3.5 / 70%) across the residency period (3.1c). A single year rated below 'Good' or any sustained adverse remark defers promotion by one year until the benchmark is restored (3.2).
