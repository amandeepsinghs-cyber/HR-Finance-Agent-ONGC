# Answer Key: P1__compare_or_so_what

**Question:** How do tenure rules differ between executive cadres and non-executive unionized employees?
**Type:** `policy` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.82s`

## Executive Synthesis
The corpus cannot support a like-for-like comparison: JRTP's tenure rules apply only to regular executives in Grades E0 through E9 (JRTP 1.2), and no transfer or tenure policy for non-executive unionised staff exists in ONGC's document set. The executive-only scope is deliberate - other rulebooks such as the Leave Rules expressly cover both executives and non-executives (Leave Rules 1.2). For executives, transfer is an incident of service anywhere in India or abroad (JRTP 1.4); non-executive transfer terms would sit in standing orders or wage settlements that are not in the corpus.
