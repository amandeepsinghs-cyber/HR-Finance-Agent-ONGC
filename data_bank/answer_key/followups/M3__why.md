# Answer Key: M3__why

**Question:** Why are individual salary and APAR appraisals classified as strictly confidential personal data?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `2` rows | **Execution:** `0.56s`

## Executive Synthesis
REFUSAL upheld: colleagues' salary and APAR ratings are protected personal information under HR Manual Clause 5.0 (Confidentiality of Individual Compensation), which invokes Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act and makes unauthorised querying major misconduct under the CDA Rules. "5.1 Colleague Data Privacy: The individual salary details, net pay, allowances, deductions, bank accounts, income tax records, and performance ratings of colleagues and supervisors are protected..." Appraisal-linked DPC deliberations are separately secret under R&P/Promotion Clause 8.0 (Confidentiality of DPC Proceedings & Sealed Cover).
