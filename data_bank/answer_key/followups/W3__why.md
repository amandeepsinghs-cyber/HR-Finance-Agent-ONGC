**Type:** `what_if` | **Result Count:** `15` rows | **Execution:** `0.67s`

## Executive Synthesis
The data shows only a moderate link: assets with more technical-executive vacancies run slightly lower uptime (correlation -0.39; with downtime 0.37). The four most short-staffed assets (Neelam & Heera, Bassein & Satellite, Eastern Offshore and Mumbai High, 11.9%-13.7% vacant) average 96.56% uptime versus 97.35% for the four best-staffed. The gap is under a percentage point and the short-staffed assets are mostly offshore, so the effect is plausible but not proven causal.
