**Question:** Why did fields with substantial 2P oil/gas reserves receive less development drilling capex than planned in the BE/RE cycles?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `15` rows | **Execution:** `0.76s`

## Executive Synthesis
The shortfall is mostly not under-spending against BE/RE but the absence of any development-drilling budget line: Bassein & Satellite (179.6 MMTOE 2P), Eastern Offshore (126.0 MMTOE 2P) and Neelam & Heera (53.0 MMTOE 2P) had no development-drilling capex budgeted or spent in FY22–FY26. Where a line exists, spend tracked plan closely - Mumbai High 95.2% of BE, Ankleshwar Asset 100.7% of BE, Mehsana Asset 96.1% of BE, CBM Bokaro 99.5% of BE and Cauvery Asset 99.4% of BE - and the largest gap is Mumbai High at ₹-232 cr vs BE. The data holds no recorded reason for the allocation, so the 'why' is a planning-allocation gap (reserves not translated into drilling budgets), not an execution shortfall; capex is also not recorded at field level.
