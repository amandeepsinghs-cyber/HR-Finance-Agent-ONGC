**Question:** Why is vigilance inquiry status confidential and exempt from disclosure?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.63s`

## Executive Synthesis
Vigilance inquiry status is confidential because the Whistle Blower Policy requires the identities of both the complainant and the subject to be kept confidential while a complaint is screened and investigated, and the subject is told of allegations only when a formal investigation opens. Officers facing proceedings are assessed under sealed cover at DPC. Disclosing whether a named officer is under inquiry would breach those safeguards. Cited: P2-01 Part 9 (Whistle Blower Policy / Vigil Mechanism, 2018 (Part 9)); P2-01 Part 12 (Whistle Blower Policy / Vigil Mechanism, 2018 (Part 12)); R&P/Promotion Clause 8.0 (Confidentiality of DPC Proceedings & Sealed Cover).
