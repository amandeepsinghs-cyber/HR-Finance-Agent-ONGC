**Question:** What is our current RTI compliance and disposal rate compared to the statutory 30-day timeline mandated by the RTI Act?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `3` rows | **Execution:** `0.62s`

## Executive Synthesis
ONGC has disposed of 89.5% of RTI applications received since FY25 (697 of 779), and 88.2% of disposals met the 30-day statutory timeline. In FY26 the compliance rate was 87.2% with an average of 22.0 days. The bigger exposure is the backlog: 63 applications are still undisposed beyond 30 days, each a potential deemed refusal under the RTI Act.
