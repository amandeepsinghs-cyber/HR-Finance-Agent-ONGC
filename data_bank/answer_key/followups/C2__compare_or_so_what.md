## Executive Synthesis
1,908 of the 8,457 overdue executives (22.6%) have never completed a mandatory 36-month tenure in a Category-C or offshore location. Of these, 1,542 served such a station for less than three years and 366 have never been posted to one at all. They are the natural first candidates for the hard-station vacancies, since JRTP Clause 6.1 bars E4→E5 promotion without that tenure.
