**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `4` rows | **Execution:** `0.75s`

## Executive Synthesis
The data does not show a persistent North-East gap — NE vacancy is 3.1% versus 7.6% for the rest of the company — the real shortfall is offshore: Eastern Offshore (KG deepwater) 12.8% and Western offshore 12.0%. Exits are not the driver (separations run at 5.1–5.6 per 100 posts offshore, similar to 5.8 elsewhere); Western offshore received only 3.6 transfer-ins per 100 posts in FY26 against 726 open posts. NE inflow was the weakest at 2.3 per 100 posts, but low NE vacancies (143) more likely reflect long incumbent stays than successful replenishment.
