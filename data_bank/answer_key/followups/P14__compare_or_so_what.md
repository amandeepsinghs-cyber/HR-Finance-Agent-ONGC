## Executive Synthesis
A drop to Very Good would cost an average E5 Manager about ₹34,508 a year in PRP and an E7 General Manager about ₹38,140. Annual PRP per head would fall from ₹2,76,065 to ₹2,41,557 (E5) and from ₹3,05,118 to ₹2,66,978 (E7), a gap of ₹3,632 per head between the two grades. E7 PRP is only 9.9% of basic pay against 12.7% for E5, which is why the senior grade's loss is not much larger; figures are FY26 averages per full-time equivalent.
