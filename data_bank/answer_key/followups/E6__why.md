## Executive Synthesis
18 of the 36 FY27 programmes require prior field experience (up to 5 years), most often in general_management (4 of 6). Prerequisites keep advanced courses for officers who can apply them on the job, so seats are not used up by people without the operating background. With 5.2 years' service you are blocked from 0 programmes. The calendar records the prerequisite but not the reason for it.
