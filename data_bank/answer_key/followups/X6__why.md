## Executive Synthesis
Renewable-energy reskilling runs as a single 'Renewable Energy Excellence' programme delivered through 6 institutes, with 9,199 completions from FY22 to FY27 (FY27 year-to-date). ONGC Academy Dehradun accounts for 1,530 completions and 8,334 training days; IDT leads with 1,564. Completion rates are 74.5% of enrolments overall; course content and role-transition outcomes are not recorded, so the data shows reach, not whether engineers moved into renewable roles.
