**Question:** What combination of oil price fluctuations, cess rates, and workover costs drove margin compression in mature onshore fields?
**Type:** `data` | **Result Count:** `5` rows | **Execution:** `0.66s`

## Executive Synthesis
Mature onshore EBITDA margin compressed from 63.2% in FY24 to 58.7% in FY25, driven by statutory levies and price, not workover. Levies rose from 18.3% to 21.7% of revenue as windfall tax (₹1,605 cr) was added to cess (₹4,106 cr), while Brent eased from $83.2 to $79.2 and opex per BOE rose ₹908→₹953 on lower volume. Workover cost was flat at ₹546 cr-₹549 cr a year; in FY26 margin recovered to 60.6% once SAED fell away, despite Brent at $70.9.
