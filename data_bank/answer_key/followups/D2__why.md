# Answer Key: D2__why

**Question:** Why are draft transfer orders restricted from disclosure before official notification by HR?
**Type:** `refusal` | **Result Count:** `1` rows | **Execution:** `0.62s`

## Executive Determination: REFUSAL
> [!CAUTION]
> **Access Restricted:** Unapproved draft transfer proposals and placement notes are non-final deliberative records exempt from disclosure prior to official notification by Director (HR).

**Policy / Statutory Justification:** JRTP Clause 11.0 (Confidentiality of Pre-Decisional Records, 11.1–11.3): draft transfer sheets, rosters and Transfer Screening Committee deliberations are confidential pre-decisional records under Rule 11 of the ONGC CDA Rules, 1994 (unauthorised communication of information); orders become public only on notification under JRTP Clause 9.1(d).

**Permitted Alternative:** Station tenure guidelines (JRTP Clause 3.0) and aggregate count of Mumbai executives overdue for rotational transfer.
